Create "Iridescence — Soap Bubbles". A soft, bright sky (#dbeafe → #fef3f2 gradient with faint drifting clouds). Bubbles of varied sizes float upward and drift on a gentle wind, wobbling as their shape oscillates; each is rendered with thin-film interference colours — swirling bands of magenta, gold, cyan and green on a mostly transparent sphere — plus a bright window-shaped specular highlight and a smaller secondary one, with the swirl animating over time (canvas gradients and noise-driven hue shifts). A tiny, elegant "bubbles popped" counter. Typography: an airy light sans title that is itself slightly iridescent.

=== DESCRIPTION ===
Iridescence is a light, airy scene: a pale sky that fades from powder blue (#dbeafe) to blush (#fef3f2) with a warm sun bloom in one corner and faint clouds drifting across. Soap bubbles of many sizes rise slowly and drift on a gentle wind, wobbling as they go; one large "hero" bubble anchors the opening frame. Each bubble is mostly transparent, with swirling bands of magenta, gold, cyan and green that strengthen toward the rim, a bright four-pane window reflection in the upper left and a smaller glint lower right. The bands keep turning slowly over time. The title "iridescence" is set in an ultralight, widely spaced sans filled with a slowly sliding pastel-iridescent gradient. A tiny counter of bubbles popped, with a spinning rainbow ring, sits in the top corner, and soft glassy pills hold the hints, a wind vane, Blow and Sound.

=== MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES ===
- Physically motivated thin-film textures: reflectance sin²(2π·n·d·cosθt/λ) with n = 1.33, sampled at paired red, green and blue wavelengths, computed per pixel at startup into five 256×256 sprites (two at boot, the rest over later frames)
- Film thickness from a gravity gradient (thin crown, thick base) plus a domain-warped, radially twisted simplex swirl; a "black film" fade where the film is thinnest, Fresnel-weighted alpha so the centre stays clear and the rim glows, and a faint sky reflection in the upper half
- Two film layers per bubble, counter-rotating and cross-fading at their own rates, so the bands appear to swirl
- Quadrupole shape wobble (squash and stretch along a turning axis) driven by a damped oscillator that collisions and air kick; smaller bubbles wobble faster
- Pre-rendered highlight sprites: a softly blurred four-pane window reflection and radial glints, placed with skew transforms
- Procedural cloud sprites built from soft radial puffs with a cool shaded underside, drifting with parallax on a separate sky canvas
- Soft pairwise collisions with mass-weighted separation and impulse, buoyant lift, and noise-driven turbulence
- Pop effect: a ring of tinted droplets with gravity and highlights, curling iridescent film fragments stroked with gradient arcs, and a fading flash ring
- The wand (lavender ring and handle, with film inside the ring) is drawn on canvas, and the new bubble inflates out of the ring along the drag direction
- DPR-aware canvases; the pastel gradient title is animated with background-clip text

=== INTERACTION MODEL ===
- Click or tap a bubble to pop it, with a synthesized "pop" (a band-passed noise burst plus a falling sine blip, lower for bigger bubbles) and a bump of the popped counter (kept in guarded localStorage)
- Press on empty sky and drag to blow a bubble from a wand: it grows with the drag distance and detaches on release, carrying the flick velocity; a quick tap releases a small bubble
- Moving the pointer steers the wind (direction and strength shown by the wind vane), which then relaxes back to a light breeze
- "Blow" button and keys B, Space or Enter blow a bubble; P pops the largest; the arrow keys push the wind
- Visible Sound/Muted toggle; audio starts only after a gesture; animation pauses while the tab is hidden; reduced motion slows drift, swirl and wobble and shows fewer bubbles
