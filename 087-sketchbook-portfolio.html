page one · hello

Hello, I’m Juno.

I’m an illustrator — a made-up one, for this page — who draws small, ordinary things until they feel a little bit magic: trams at night, soup, plants that refuse to die, lighthouses, sleepy cats.

This sketchbook is my portfolio. The finished work is in here, and so are the coffee rings.

currently: drawing every bench in the park. 41 so far.
