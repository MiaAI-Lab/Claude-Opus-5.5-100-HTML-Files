§ 02P. 04–09

New Releases

Six records. Zero filler. Every sleeve is generated fresh — no two pressings look alike.

PRESSING #001
LEAD RELEASE

LHR-071 · LP · OUT 09 OCT 2026

Concrete Lullabies

Mirror Static

Eight songs about ringroads, overpasses and the specific loneliness of a car alarm at 3am. Recorded live to tape in a disused multistorey, bass so dry you could strike a match on it.

  • POST-PUNK
  • 124 BPM
  • A MINOR
  • 180G BLACK

LHR-072 · 12" · OUT 16 OCT 2026

Sodium Heart

Dolly Voltage

Streetlight synth-pop with a dub on the flip. Four tracks, one broken drum machine, zero regrets.

  • SYNTH-POP
  • 118 BPM
  • F♯ MINOR

LHR-073 · LP · OUT 23 OCT 2026

Basement Gospel

The Parking Lots

Seven garage hymns at a tempo your landlord will object to. The amp broke on track one. They kept going.

  • GARAGE
  • 142 BPM
  • A MAJOR

LHR-074 · CASSETTE · OUT 30 OCT 2026

Tape Hiss Forever

Kid Cassette

A beat tape bounced down to chrome, then played in a warm car for a week. The hiss is the point.

  • LO-FI BEATS
  • 88 BPM
  • C MINOR

LHR-075 · 7" SINGLE · OUT 06 NOV 2026

Riot in C Minor

Gravel & Honey

Two minutes fifty-two of noise-pop with a chorus you will hum at a funeral. Sorry in advance.

  • NOISE-POP
  • 160 BPM
  • C MINOR

LHR-076 · 2×LP · OUT 13 NOV 2026

Slow Strobe

Outlet Sisters

Four sides of dub techno, one track per side, each long enough to miss your stop on the night bus.

  • DUB TECHNO
  • 128 BPM
  • G MINOR
