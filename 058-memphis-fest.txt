=== DESCRIPTION ===
RADICAL SHAPES is a landing page for a fictional festival, the Milano Design Fest '86, designed as if the Memphis movement had made its own website. Every block has a thick black outline and a hard offset shadow, and the page sits on a 12-column grid, so the loud patterns stay organised. The hero stacks the chunky geometric title (black-stroked letters with two-colour offset shadows) over a tilted terrazzo slab on graph paper, with a dotted turquoise disc, a squiggle band and a dozen hand-drawn Memphis objects floating around it: a striped cylinder, a zigzag, a dotted triangle, a half-moon, a banded sphere, a totem column, a lightning bolt and a rainbow arch. Below the hero come a black countdown band with yellow flip-card digits and saw-tooth edges; six designer cards with patterned headers and geometric portraits; a lilac graph-paper schedule with day tabs and a shaped-dot timeline; an illustrated map of a made-up district drawn from chunky SVG blocks; and three perforated tickets on a terrazzo pink field.

=== MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES ===
- Nine SVG <pattern> tiles (terrazzo, confetti, squiggle, zigzag, polka dots, stripes, graph grid, dotted triangles, rainbow waves) inked through CSS custom properties, so a single variable swap recolours every pattern at once
- Offset-shadow Memphis styling everywhere: 3px ink borders, hard box-shadows, black-stroked text with paint-order and two-colour text-shadows, tilted blocks and taped cards
- A spring-physics layer for the hero shapes: each shape has a home position, velocity, damping, idle bobbing and rotation, and is pushed away by the cursor with a squash-and-stretch wobble
- The Memphis shapes are drawn at runtime as small parameterised inline SVGs; new ones pop in with an overshooting scale-and-rotate plus a CSS confetti burst
- 3D flip-clock digits (split halves plus a rotating leaf with backface-visibility) driven by a real countdown to the next 14 April, 18:00 Milan time, computed with Intl time zones
- Illustrated SVG map with offset-shadow blocks, a dashed-road grid, a canal, lollipop trees and venues that lift on hover or focus and show a dashed selection ring
- Ticket stubs with perforation lines and half-circle notches, a rotated "Most radical" badge, IntersectionObserver reveal-on-scroll, and a toast that slides up with an overshoot

=== INTERACTION MODEL ===
- Move the cursor (or drag a finger) through the hero and the floating shapes bounce away, then spring back home
- Click or tap anywhere in the hero to drop a new random Memphis shape with a pop and confetti (the oldest disappear after 18)
- Cards wobble on hover or keyboard focus; the letters of the title hop when hovered
- The Shuffle button (or the S key) reassigns every background pattern and rotates the pattern ink palette across the whole page
- Schedule tabs (arrow keys supported, ARIA tablist) switch between five days; each day changes the theme card, its illustration and a staggered timeline
- Venue blocks on the map are keyboard-focusable buttons; choosing one updates the info panel beside the map
- Ticket "Reserve" buttons answer with a toast explaining that the festival is fictional; sticky nav links jump to each section
- prefers-reduced-motion stops the bobbing, rotation, wobble, reveal and flip animations; the animation loop pauses when the tab is hidden or the hero is off-screen
