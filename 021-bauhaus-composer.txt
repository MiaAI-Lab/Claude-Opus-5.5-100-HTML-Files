=== DESCRIPTION ===
"formlehre — bauhaus composer" is a tactile composition toy laid out like a printed Bauhaus sheet: a deep-cream paper page with a heavy black rule under an oversized lowercase masthead, and five-plus numbered sections whose giant numerals each sit on a coloured primary form (1 tray, 2 board, 3 colour, 4 actions, 5 notes, 6 balance). The centre of the page is a warm cream board (#f1ead8) with a hard black offset shadow and chess-style a–h / 1–8 edge labels; forms in red #d62d20, yellow #f4c20d, blue #1d4e9e and black #111 are dragged onto it from a tray, caught by a hidden 8×8 grid with a springy settle, and edited with handles, swatches and geometric toolbar buttons. A "kandinsky" button composes a balanced arrangement from his colour–form pairings, and a live balance map shows where the composition's visual weight sits. All headings and UI copy are lowercase in the spirit of Herbert Bayer's universal alphabet, set in a geometric sans stack (Futura, Century Gothic, Avenir Next, URW Gothic).

=== MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES ===
- Board rendered as an 800-unit SVG; every form is a single path (circle, square, equilateral triangle, semicircle, quarter circle, bar) placed with translate / rotate / scale, so position, angle and size can all be sprung independently
- Damped-spring physics (semi-implicit Euler, 120 Hz substeps) for position, rotation and size: drops overshoot and settle, recolours and size changes "pulse", deleted forms shrink away, Kandinsky compositions pop in with a staggered cascade
- Grid snapping by bounding-box parity (odd-sized forms centre on cells, even-sized on grid lines, bars ride the lines) with rotated-bbox handling; the hidden grid and a dashed snap preview fade in only while dragging
- Selection frame with non-scaling dashed stroke, four resize handles and a yellow rotate knob, all kept a constant on-screen size via a units-per-pixel factor
- Printed-paper feel: a runtime-generated noise tile multiplied over the page and over the board (so the flat colours read as ink), a soft top-left paper sheen, hard 11px black offset shadows, flat drop-shadows on lifted forms
- Numeral section markers built from CSS pseudo-elements clipped into triangle, square, circle, semicircle and quarter-circle shapes behind heavy digits; toolbar and swatches drawn as coloured geometric SVG buttons
- Balance map: a miniature ghost of the board with a crosshair at the centre of visual weight (area × colour heaviness) that springs into place, plus a stacked colour-weight bar
- PNG export drawn to a 1600px canvas with Path2D from the same path data, with a black rule and lowercase caption band

=== INTERACTION MODEL ===
- Drag a form from the tray (mouse or touch) onto the board; a ghost follows the pointer at true board scale and lands where you drop it, then springs to the snapped cell. Tap or press Enter on a tray tile to place it in the emptiest spot
- Drag forms on the board to move them; drop one outside the board to remove it
- Rotate with the mouse wheel (15° steps), the R key (45°, Shift+R back), the "turn" button, or by dragging the yellow knob (15° snaps); resize with the corner handles, + / − keys or buttons
- Click a swatch to recolour the selected form; with nothing selected the swatch recolours the tray (click again to return to Kandinsky's pairings). Keys 1–4 recolour too
- Toolbar: turn, larger, smaller, front, copy, remove, undo, clear, plus "kandinsky" (also the K key) and "png" export
- Keyboard: Tab to a form to select it, arrows nudge by one cell, Delete/Backspace removes, F brings to front, D duplicates, Escape deselects, Ctrl/Cmd+Z undoes (80-step history)
- The composition and composition number persist in localStorage (guarded with try/catch); first visit opens on a seeded Kandinsky composition
- The live readout names the selected form in German and English with its grid span, angle and size; prefers-reduced-motion switches the springs to critically damped and removes the stagger
