=== DESCRIPTION ===
Horologe is a typographic word clock that spells the current time on an original 11×10 letter grid: "IT IS TWENTY FIVE TO TEN", to five-minute precision, with four corner dots adding the single minutes. The English layout is my own arrangement. It adds NOON and MIDNIGHT for 12:00, and its seven filler letters secretly spell MOMENTS. The second-language layout is an original Spanish grid ("SON LAS NUEVE Y MEDIA", "ES LA UNA MENOS CUARTO", "… EN PUNTO") whose fillers spell MOMENTO. Letters are set in a clean geometric sans on a grid that spaces them widely. Inactive letters are faint (#2a2a2a on #111) and active ones glow warm white (#fff6e5) with a soft bloom; they fade off and on in a gentle, reading-order stagger when the time changes. Four faces restyle everything, including the page, controls and dots: Obsidian (black glass with a diagonal reflection), Paper (letterpress ink on #f3efe6), Copper (letters etched into a brushed-copper plate, lit ones backlit and glowing) and Neon (magenta tubes on a dark brick wall, flickering as they strike). Beneath the face, a serif subtitle gives the date in words ("Tuesday, the twenty-second of September") with the year spelled out. The face always stays square and scales to fill the screen.

=== MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES ===
- 110 letter cells in a CSS grid sized from a single --S variable (min of viewport width and height), so the square face and its type scale together on any screen.
- Every face is a set of CSS custom properties on <body> (page background, panel gradient, sheen, off/on colours, layered text-shadow bloom, radius), so a face switch is one attribute change with smooth colour transitions.
- Stagger choreography: letters switching off fade first, then new letters light in reading order via per-cell transition-delay; minute dots follow in clockwise order.
- Language change as a diagonal flip wave: each cell folds away on rotateX with a delay from row plus column, the letters are rewritten while hidden, then they fold back in.
- A brushed-copper plate from stacked repeating-linear-gradients over a multi-stop copper gradient, with an inset bevel and etched off-letters (paired light/dark text-shadows).
- Neon tube ignition: a keyframed flicker on each newly lit letter (fill-mode backwards, so it never blocks the flip), a magenta ambient glow and a brick wall made of repeating gradients.
- A glass sheen for Obsidian, letterpress deboss for Paper, and a demo progress hairline with a monospace readout.

=== INTERACTION MODEL ===
- Click, tap or press Enter on the clock (it is a real button) to switch between the English and Spanish layouts; the EN/ES pill and the L key do the same. The date and year switch language too.
- Face switcher with four swatches (keys 1–4); the choice and language are remembered in localStorage (guarded with try/catch).
- Demo (D) runs a whole day, 00:00 to 23:59, in 20 seconds, with fast transitions, a progress hairline and a digital readout, then returns to the real time. Pressing it again stops early.
- Fullscreen (F) with auto-hiding chrome and cursor after 2.2 s of stillness.
- M reveals the hidden word in the filler letters, one letter at a time.
- The face's accessible label and a polite live region give the time in words (including the extra minutes); updates pause while the tab is hidden; prefers-reduced-motion shortens the fades and removes the flicker and flip.
