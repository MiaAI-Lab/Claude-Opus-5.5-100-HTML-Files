In the Greek myth, Daedalus built the Labyrinth for King Minos of Crete, and Ariadne gave Theseus a ball of thread so he could find his way back out. Each labyrinth here is a perfect maze, carved by a recursive backtracker, so exactly one path joins any two chambers.
