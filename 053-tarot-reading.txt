=== DESCRIPTION ===
"Arcana Nocturne" is a three-card tarot reading staged on a midnight-purple velvet table under drifting gold and silver star dust. A faint astrological wheel turns slowly behind three dashed slots (Past, Present, Future) marked with a crescent, a sun and a star. On load the reader spreads the deck: 22 card backs, each an intricate symmetric design of a sunburst eye inside a gilded medallion with crescents, a lattice ground and fleuron corners, fan out in a gentle arc. Drawing a card lifts it from the fan and flies it to its slot, where it turns over in 3D with a glint of light to reveal one of 22 original, procedurally drawn Major Arcana faces in gold foil and moonlight silver (a lightning-struck tower, a moon between two towers, an eight-pointed star over water, and so on). Each face has an arched night window, a numeral cartouche and a title plate. Under each card appears an engraved small-caps name, its orientation and an original italic interpretation. After the third card the deck gathers away and a single line ties the reading together. The whole page is framed as entertainment.

=== MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES ===
- 22 card faces built at runtime as inline SVG from hand-written motif functions (sunbursts, eight-pointed stars, crescents, lemniscates, waves, flowers, towers, scales, wreaths) sharing one set of userSpaceOnUse gold-foil and silver gradients, so the foil sheen sweeps consistently across every card
- Ornate frame per face: triple gold border with a dotted rail, fleuron corner symbols, an arched window clipped per card with star specks, spandrel stars, numeral cartouche and title plate; letters inside motifs (B and J, T A R O) are drawn as stroked glyph paths
- Numerals and titles are HTML overlays scaled from the card-width custom property with gold gradient text, so they stay crisp and correctly placed through the 3D flip
- Card back as a reusable SVG symbol: a 32-point sunburst computed at runtime, an eye with a silver iris, mirrored crescents, a diamond lattice pattern and corner fleurons
- Each card is a nested 3D stack (position, hover lift, pointer tilt, flip) with backface-visibility faces, a CSS glint sweep after the flip, and a pointer-following shimmer blended with screen
- Web Animations API choreography: gather, split into two bent piles, a staggered riffle into a Fisher–Yates order, squaring taps, an arced fan layout, arced card flights with a scale "pop", and the fan re-closing around each drawn card
- Canvas star dust with twinkle, slow drift, glowing larger motes and pointer parallax; paused while the tab is hidden
- Velvet tablecloth from layered radial gradients and fine crossed pile lines, with a dashed gold embroidery inset; a slowly rotating astrological wheel drawn with dash-array tricks (ticks, house lines and dots from single circles)
- Foil title lettering with an animated gradient, small caps with generous tracking, italic serif body text, and notched gold-bordered buttons made with clip-path and a masked gradient border

=== INTERACTION MODEL ===
- Click or tap any card in the fan to draw it; cards are laid in order as Past, Present and Future, each flipping on arrival and revealing its interpretation
- Keyboard: the fan is a single tab stop with a roving focus; Left, Right, Home and End move between cards, Enter or Space draws
- "Shuffle the deck" returns any laid cards face down, gathers everything, and riffles the deck with a visible split and interleave; "Fan the cards" (or tapping the deck) spreads it again
- "Reversals" toggle (remembered in localStorage, guarded) lets roughly a third of draws land reversed, which rotates the face and switches to the reversed reading
- Hovering a laid card tilts it in 3D toward the pointer with a moving shimmer; fan cards lift and glow on hover or focus
- After three cards a summary sentence combines the three keywords; on phones the readings stack below the table
- Optional Web Audio sound (off by default, visible toggle): paper flicks while shuffling and a soft bell as each card turns
- Status line and aria-live readings announce each step; prefers-reduced-motion removes the flights, riffle, glint, drifting dust and wheel rotation
