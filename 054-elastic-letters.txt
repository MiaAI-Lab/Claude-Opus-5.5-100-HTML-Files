TITLE: ELASTIC — Letterforms on Springs
FILE: 054-elastic-letters.html

=== PROMPT (exact prompt used) ===
Build "ELASTIC — Letterforms on Springs", an experimental typography playground. A giant word (default "BOUNCE", editable via an input) is converted into points sampled from its glyphs (draw the text on an offscreen canvas and sample filled pixels); each point is tied to its home position by a spring. Render the points as connected dots/filaments forming the letters. The pointer repels (or attracts — toggle) points with a force field; releasing lets them spring back with overshoot and jiggle. Click to explode the word into particles that reassemble. Palette: hot magenta #ff2e88 on deep ink #111018, with cyan #29e7cd on fast-moving points (velocity-coloured). Modes: dots; lines (connecting nearby neighbours); threads (points tied to home with visible springs). Sliders: stiffness, damping, force radius. UI typography in small monospace; the display lettering is generated from the heavy system sans. Responsive: sampling density adapts to screen size for performance.

=== DESCRIPTION ===
ELASTIC is a dark typographic playground in deep ink (#111018), hot magenta (#ff2e88) and cyan (#29e7cd). A giant word, BOUNCE by default and editable in the dock, is drawn in the heaviest system sans on an offscreen canvas and sampled into a hexagonal lattice of roughly 2,500 points, each tied to its home by a damped spring. At rest the letters are magenta dot-matrix glyphs sitting between dashed cap-height and baseline guides, with sidebearing ticks and a per-glyph point count ("B · 477") underneath like a type specimen. As soon as anything moves, velocity turns points cyan, short comet tails form, and a soft CSS-blurred bloom makes the whole word glow. On load the points fly in from a scattered ring, then a ghost pointer combs once through the word to show the spring jiggle before handing control to you. The UI is a restrained monospace dock: word field, render mode, repel/attract, three physics sliders and a magenta Explode button.

=== MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES ===
- Glyph sampling: each character is drawn separately with custom tracking on an offscreen canvas, its alpha is read with getImageData, and a hexagonal grid is sampled; the pitch is solved from the filled area so the point budget adapts to screen size (about 700 to 3,400 points)
- Portrait screens split the word into two rows so the letters stay large on phones
- Spring physics in typed arrays (position, velocity and home as Float32Array), two semi-implicit substeps per frame, with stiffness, damping, a quadratic-falloff pointer field and a drag term from the pointer's own velocity
- A slow travelling "breathing" wave on the home positions keeps the word alive while idle
- Velocity colouring by counting-sorting points into seven magenta-to-cyan bins, one batched path per bin
- Lines mode: precomputed hex-lattice neighbour links, binned by stretch and speed, that tear (disappear) past 3.2× their rest length
- Threads mode: faint home anchors plus zig-zag coil springs drawn from home to point, which turn cyan when strongly stretched
- Explosion: radial impulses with angular jitter while spring strength drops to zero and then ramps back, so the word reassembles with overshoot
- A translucent clear gives motion trails; a quarter-resolution copy under CSS blur plus a screen blend gives the bloom; a separate HUD canvas draws the animated force-field ring, direction arrows and label
- Typographic guides (cap height, baseline, sidebearings, glyph point counts) drawn from real text metrics

=== INTERACTION MODEL ===
- Hover (mouse) or drag (touch) to push the points; switch the pointer to Attract to pull them instead (key A)
- Click or tap without dragging to explode the word from that spot; the Explode button (or E / Space) bursts it from the centre
- Type a new word (up to 10 characters): the points morph from the old word into the new one
- Render modes Dots, Lines and Threads (keys 1–3); sliders for stiffness, damping and force radius, with live readouts
- Synthesized Web Audio "boing" on explode and a soft pluck on mode or word changes, created only after a gesture, with a visible Sound/Muted toggle
- Word, mode, sliders and sound preference are remembered with guarded localStorage; the loop pauses while the tab is hidden; reduced motion skips the intro scatter, ghost pass, trails and breathing
