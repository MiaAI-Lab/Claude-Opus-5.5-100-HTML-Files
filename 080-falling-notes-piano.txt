=== DESCRIPTION ===
A nocturnal, immersive piano visualizer. A deep #0a0a12 stage fills the screen above a realistic three-octave keyboard (C3–C6, 37 keys) with a lacquered fallboard and a thin red felt line. Glowing rounded bars fall toward the keys, each coloured by pitch class on a soft chromatic rainbow (rose C through orange, lime, cyan and indigo to magenta B). When a bar lands, its key sinks and glows, a light beam rises from it, and a burst of sparks and an elliptical bloom flare up. Faint key lanes, octave marks, drifting dust and a violet horizon glow give the stage depth. A floating glass control bar carries the rainbow small-caps "Luminote" wordmark, the three modes, a melody picker, play/restart, a tempo slider, a label toggle and mute. The page opens in Listen mode, already raining the opening of Für Elise silently, with a breathing "Tap anywhere to wake the piano" pill.

=== MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES ===
- A DPR-aware canvas stage over a pre-rendered backdrop (gradient, lane shading, octave labels). Additive ("lighter") compositing for bars, beams, blooms and sparks. Bars are rounded rects with a vertical gradient, a pitch-coloured shadowBlur halo and a bright inner core.
- A pooled spark system (420 particles, gravity, drag, streak rendering), key light beams that fade on release, radial bloom bursts, twinkling dust motes and a rainbow horizon line at the keys.
- CSS-built keys. Ivory keys use layered gradients with a front lip (inset shadows); black keys have a glossy highlight strip and a raised lip. Pressed states translate the key, shrink the lip and cast a glow in the note's colour (a CSS custom property per key). Learn-mode hints pulse, and wrong notes shake.
- Web Audio piano synthesis: eight sine partials with slight detune and inharmonic stretch, per-partial decays, a 5 ms attack, pitch-dependent exponential decay, a lowpass whose cutoff falls over time, a filtered-noise hammer tick, a convolver reverb from a generated stereo noise impulse (with sparse early reflections and a darkening tail), and a gentle compressor.
- A glass UI with backdrop blur, a gradient-text wordmark, small-caps labels and a rainbow progress bar.

=== INTERACTION MODEL ===
- Play with mouse or touch: multi-touch chords work, and sliding across keys plays a glissando. On a computer keyboard, the bottom row Z…/ (with S D G H J L ;) starts at C3 and the top row Q…] (with 2 3 5 6 7 9 0 =) starts at C4. Pressing near the front of a key plays slightly louder.
- Free play: every note you play rises from its key as a ribbon of light that grows while held and floats away on release.
- Listen: auto-plays Ode to Joy, the opening of Für Elise, Twinkle Twinkle Little Star, or the opening of Satie's Gymnopédie No. 1 (with its alternating G/D major-seventh accompaniment). Notes fall in time and loop "da capo". Play/pause and restart are available.
- Learn: the melody falls and stops when the next note reaches the keyboard. That key glows, and the coach shows its name and computer key until you play it. Wrong notes shake, a first-try accuracy score is kept and a result card appears at the end.
- The tempo slider (40–160%) keeps the fall speed constant in pixels while changing the pace. Labels switch between note names, computer-key letters and off, and are remembered with tempo and mute in localStorage (guarded).
- Audio starts on the first gesture, and the mute button fades the master output. On phones, a compact bar hides the extra settings behind a toggle. The stage and keyboard scroll sideways together, auto-following the upcoming notes unless you scroll yourself. Reduced motion trims sparks and dust.
