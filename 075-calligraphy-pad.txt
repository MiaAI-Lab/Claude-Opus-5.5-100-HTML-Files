=== DESCRIPTION ===
Brush & Ink is a quiet, lamp-lit calligraphy desk. A sheet of rice paper with a deckled edge, drifting fibres and faint laid lines lies on a dark procedurally grained wooden desk; to its left a lacquer-dark tool rail with vertical 筆墨 lettering, to its right an illustrated inkstone with an ink stick, a bamboo brush on a porcelain rest and a carved red seal. On load the brush writes 永 (eternity), the character used to teach the "eight principles of yong", stroke by stroke, followed by a small vertical inscription giving the current season and year in the sexagenary cycle, and the seal. From then on the paper is yours: strokes swell when you move slowly and thin when you move fast, respond to pen pressure, taper at the ends, bleed a soft halo into the paper and run dry over long strokes into streaky "flying white".

=== MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES ===
- A custom brush engine that stamps along each stroke at sub-pixel spacing: width follows smoothed speed (slow = thick) and pen pressure, eases in at the entry, and tapers into a tail whose length depends on exit speed
- Dry brush: 26 simulated bristles with their own dryness bias and low-frequency noise; as the stroke's ink falls, the solid core shrinks and only bristles with enough ink leave marks, producing parallel streaks of flying white; bristle normals are smoothed so turns do not fan out
- Ink bleed: every wet stamp also paints a larger, low-alpha disc on a separate canvas shown through a CSS blur, giving a subtle feathered halo that is stronger for wetter ink
- Layered canvases (paper, grid, trace, bleed, live stroke, ink); the live stroke is drawn opaque and composited at the ink's alpha on release, so diluted grey stays even within a stroke but darkens where strokes overlap
- Procedural rice paper: soft mottling, thousands of curved light and dark fibres, specks, laid lines, edge tint and a randomly jittered clip-path polygon for the deckled edge
- Procedural wooden desk: planks of wavy grain lines that swerve around knots, seams, and a lamp-glow vignette
- The model 永 is defined as five stroke skeletons (dot, horizontal-turn-hook, horizontal-sweep, sweep, press) with per-point pacing, interpolated with Catmull-Rom splines and written by the same engine, so the model has true brush modulation
- Seal carving: seeded, rectilinear pseudo-characters in a 2×2 layout cut out of a vermilion square, with edge wear and speckle, rendered as SVG for the preview and to canvas for stamping
- Vertical CJK accents set with writing-mode: vertical-rl in a Kai-style font stack, set against small italic Garamond-style labels

=== INTERACTION MODEL ===
- Draw with mouse, finger or pen (coalesced pointer events for smooth curves); a ring cursor shows the brush footprint on desktop
- Ink wells choose sumi black, diluted grey or vermilion (radio group with arrow-key support); the brush illustration's tip darkens with the chosen ink and fades as the stroke's ink runs out
- Sliders set brush size and ink wetness; wetter ink lasts longer and bleeds more
- Undo (button or Ctrl/Cmd+Z) removes the last stroke, seal or inscription by replaying the recorded strokes exactly, with the same seeds
- Clear slides the sheet off the desk and slides a fresh sheet, with new fibres, in from the other side
- Trace shows the faint model 永 in a large practice square with numbered stroke order; Grid toggles faint vermilion practice squares (twelve small squares, or the large one while tracing)
- Seal: pick it up from the rail or the desk, see a ghost follow the pointer, click to press it onto the paper; "carve another" generates a new seal
- Save downloads a PNG composed of paper, grid, trace, blurred bleed and ink
- Clicking the inkstone grinds fresh ink with a ripple; settings persist in guarded localStorage; the drawing re-renders at the new size when the window changes; reduced motion writes the opening character instantly and skips the sliding animations
