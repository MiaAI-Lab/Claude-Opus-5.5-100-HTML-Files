=== DESCRIPTION ===
"Senbazuru — Fold a Paper Crane" folds a real, physically consistent paper crane in the browser. A square of washi (indigo #264653 pattern on cream, coral #e76f51 reverse) is simulated as rigid facets hinged along true crease lines. It passes through eight stages (square, triangle, square base by reverse-folding the corners, front petal fold, back petal fold / bird base, narrowing, inside-reverse-folded neck and tail, then a reverse-folded head and opening wings) and each fold animates as hinge rotations that keep the paper attached. Front and back faces are always correct, so the coral reverse appears exactly where real paper would show it: the lifted petals and the wings. The layout is Japanese-minimal on off-white #f7f3ec with plenty of space: a huge hairline-outline step numeral, the step name in italic serif, one line of instruction, quiet circular controls, three washi swatches, and an eight-dot progress rail under the model. A pale coral sun sits behind the paper, and a soft ground shadow sits beneath it. At the finale the crane flaps gently, floats and slowly turns, and a counter reads "1 of 1000" (it keeps counting cranes folded in this browser).

=== MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES ===
- A small rigid-origami engine: each facet is a paper-space polygon with a 3x4 rigid transform; every fold splits the facets it crosses and rotates the moving side by pi about the crease line (60 facets by the end)
- Folds are recorded as timed hinge rotations and composed in order during playback (kite folds then petal lift, back then front, neck then tail), so adjacent facets stay joined throughout the motion rather than being morphed
- Layer order is tracked explicitly (reverse folds tuck flaps between layers) and converted to tiny per-layer offsets along the paper normal so stacked paper sorts correctly
- Rendered with CSS 3D: every facet is two backface-hidden elements (washi front, coral back) with a matrix3d transform, a clip-path polygon grown ~0.6px to hide seams, and the browser's own 3D depth sorting
- Procedural washi painted onto canvases: seigaiha waves, asanoha hemp-leaf star lattice and shippo interlocking circles, all set at 45 degrees to the diamond, plus paper fibres and a faint edge line so stacked layers read clearly; each facet samples its own region, so patterns stay continuous across folds
- Per-facet directional lighting: the facet normal is taken through the camera rotation and dotted with a key light; the result drives a dark or warm-light overlay, so each facet's shade changes live with its angle
- Camera direction per stage (it swings round behind the model for the back petal fold, then returns), auto-fitted to each stage's bounding box, with gentle idle sway
- Finale: wings flap about their true hinge line, the crane bobs above a shadow that shrinks and fades as it rises, and it turns slowly like a hanging mobile
- Outline numeral via -webkit-text-stroke, wide-tracked micro labels, a hand-drawn SVG hanko seal with a crane silhouette

=== INTERACTION MODEL ===
- Auto-play starts shortly after load (not with reduced motion) and loops: fold, pause on each stage, fly for a while, then unfold and begin again; Pause/Auto-play toggle and the P or Space key control it
- Previous / next buttons, Left/Right arrow keys, Home (unfold to the square) and End (jump to the crane); clicking a rail dot animates quickly through the intermediate folds to that step
- Drag (mouse or touch) on the stage to orbit with inertia; double-click resets the view; with the stage focused, W A S D orbit and 0 resets
- Washi picker (radio group, arrow keys supported) repaints the paper's textures instantly, mid-fold if you like; the choice persists in localStorage
- The "of 1000" crane counter persists in localStorage (all storage access guarded with try/catch)
- prefers-reduced-motion: no auto-play, shorter folds, no sway, turning, bobbing or flapping; animation pauses while the tab is hidden
