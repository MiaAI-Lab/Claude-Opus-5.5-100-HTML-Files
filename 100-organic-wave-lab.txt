A full-screen 2D wave simulation (discrete wave equation on a grid with damping and absorbing boundaries) rendered with gorgeous shading: a water-like surface lit from above with normal-based highlights, coloured from aqua #3ee6d6 through deep teal #0b4f6c with coral #ff7b6b crests (plus palettes "Lagoon", "Bioluminescent" and "Monochrome ink"). A lab-instrument UI panel (glassy, rounded, organic shapes) with sliders for wave speed, damping and source frequency, plus a probe that plots amplitude over time at a point as a live oscilloscope trace. Performance: a Float32Array grid at reduced resolution, drawn via ImageData and smoothed. Typography: a rounded scientific sans.

=== DESCRIPTION ===
A full-screen ripple tank that feels like a luminous physics instrument. The whole viewport is a live water surface solved from the discrete 2D wave equation and lit from above: deep teal troughs (#0b4f6c), aqua slopes (#3ee6d6), coral-tinted crests (#ff7b6b), sharp specular glints and soft caustic brightening where waves focus. It opens mid-experiment. A glowing wave paddle sends plane waves into a pearl-white barrier, and two slits spread them into a fan of interference fringes with clear nodal lines. Two organic, asymmetric-cornered glass instruments float above the water. On the left is the lab console (tools, experiments, medium, oscillators, palette, actions). At the bottom right is a probe oscilloscope, joined by an animated dashed "lead" to a crosshair probe in the water. Three palettes restyle both the water and the glass: Lagoon, Bioluminescent (electric blue on midnight with cyan-green crests) and Monochrome ink (an ink-wash print on warm paper). On phones the tank runs in portrait (waves travel downward), the console becomes a bottom sheet and the scope docks top-right.

=== MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES ===
- Simulation: Float32Array leapfrog integration of the wave equation on a reduced grid (about 62k cells, sized to the screen). A per-cell speed factor creates the lens region (n ≈ 1.54). Walls act as reflecting cells. Global damping acts on velocity, and a quadratic sponge layer forms the absorbing boundaries. Three substeps run per frame, and presets are pre-warmed so the first frame already shows developed interference.
- Rendering: a per-pixel pass writes ImageData at grid resolution, which the browser upscales smoothly (bilinear) to the DPR-sized canvas. Normals from central differences give Lambert diffuse light and a Blinn specular term (^64). Negative-Laplacian "caustic" brightening marks focusing crests, and softsign tone mapping on height and slope keeps faint diffracted ripples visible without blowing out standing waves. Palette LUTs are 256-entry smoothstep ramps, lens cells get a subtle tint, and walls are bevel-shaded (lit and shadowed edges).
- A vector overlay canvas draws pulsing source glows with a phase-breathing ring, the wave paddle, the selection ring with a frequency/phase label, the dashed lens outline with its index label, the dish focus marker, the probe crosshair, a flowing bezier lead to the scope, and tool cursors.
- Oscilloscope: a DPR-aware canvas with an instrument grid, a glowing auto-gained trace from a 3-second ring buffer, and live PEAK / FREQ (from zero-crossings over the active window) / RMS readouts.
- Glass UI with backdrop blur and saturation, uneven border-radii for an organic pebble shape, a top-left highlight sheen, rounded-sans labels, gradient-filled range tracks and palette-driven CSS custom properties.

=== INTERACTION MODEL ===
- Tools (buttons or keys 1–5). Drop: click or drag to release droplets. Source: click to place an oscillator, click to select, drag to move, Delete to remove. Wall: drag to draw barriers. Erase: drag to open gaps. Probe: drag the oscilloscope's measuring point.
- Experiments: Double slit, Single slit, Two sources (point-source interference), Lens (a slow-wave region that focuses plane waves), Parabolic dish (plane waves reflect to a marked focus) and Rain (random droplets). Each rebuilds the tank and adapts to landscape or portrait orientation.
- Sliders: wave speed, damping, and source frequency (all sources, or only the selected one) plus phase for the selected source, with live readouts in tank-Hz. Waves visibly change wavelength and interference spacing as you drag.
- Actions: Pause/Play (also Space), Calm (clear the water, also C), Empty (remove all walls and sources for free experiments), and Snapshot (downloads a PNG of the tank with its overlay).
- Palette switching restyles the water, glass, scope and theme colour, and is remembered in localStorage (guarded). User walls and sources are stored in normalized coordinates and re-rasterized on resize. On phones the bottom sheet's handle expands the full console. Reduced motion lowers the simulation rate, and the loop runs on requestAnimationFrame so it pauses in background tabs.
