=== DESCRIPTION ===
Planet Forge is a procedural world generator styled as a deep-space survey console. The left stage is a cosmic black field with a painted nebula and seeded starfield, where a slowly rotating planet is shaded per pixel: fractal terrain mapped to biomes, drifting clouds, a soft day/night terminator with sparkling city lights on the night side, an atmospheric rim and halo, and optional rings that cast a real shadow band across the globe while the planet shadows the rings in turn. Moons orbit on faint traced ellipses and pass in front of and behind the planet. A survey record in the top-left gives each world a catalogue designation ("JH-4471 e"), a class, playful but plausible stats and a fictional field note; the right-hand console of thin cyan (#4cc9f0) lines and clipped-corner glass panels holds presets, sliders, toggles, seed and export.

=== MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES ===
- Seeded 3D Perlin noise sampled on the unit sphere (no seams or pole pinching) into 512x256 equirectangular fields: domain-warped fBm heights with a ridged mountain term, moisture, cloud and settlement fields; sea level is set by a latitude-weighted height quantile so the slider maps to real ocean coverage
- Biome colouring per world type (terrestrial, ocean, desert, magma, cryogenic, banded gas giant) from temperature (latitude + altitude lapse) and moisture, with hill-shade relief, smooth sea-ice/snow transitions, magma seas with floating crust, and a gas giant with warped bands, latitude-dependent wind shear and a great storm
- Ray-sphere rendering in a low-resolution buffer: per-pixel normals, texture coordinates, diffuse, night factor, Fresnel rim, Blinn-Phong ocean glint and ring-shadow factors are precomputed for the current tilt, so each frame is only texture sampling (bilinear) plus lighting, then smoothly upscaled
- Coastlines are cut along the bilinearly interpolated height contour inside mixed land/sea texels, giving antialiased sub-texel shorelines; clouds are bilinear and drift faster than the ground; city lights get a sub-texel hash sparkle
- Rings rendered in the same pass by ray-plane intersection with a seeded band profile (including a Cassini-style gap), correct depth against the sphere, lit/unlit face shading, the planet's shadow on the rings and the rings' shadow on the planet
- Moons are pre-rendered cratered sprites lit from the same sun, depth-sorted around the planet; a rotating ticked HUD arc, corner brackets and a canvas nebula with additive wisps complete the frame
- Sci-fi console: clip-path panels with diagonal corner accents, diamond slider thumbs with glow, tick-marked tracks, square toggles and steppers

=== INTERACTION MODEL ===
- Drag to spin the planet (with inertia); with a mouse, vertical drag tilts the view (re-projecting rings and shadows); arrow keys rotate/tilt when the canvas is focused; wheel or +/- zooms
- Hovering the globe reads out latitude, longitude, biome and elevation or depth under the cursor; hovering the rings reports the particle band
- Presets: Earthlike, Desert, Ocean, Lava, Ice, Gas giant; sliders for sea level, temperature, terrain roughness, cloud cover and rotation speed; toggles for rings and a survey grid (15-degree graticule drawn in the shader); moon stepper 0-4
- Seed field + Forge regenerates the world and its designation; Randomize (or R) rolls a new type, seed and parameters; PNG (or D) exports the current view with a caption; G toggles the grid
- Expensive regeneration is batched through a dirty-flag pump so dragging sliders stays responsive; reduced-motion slows rotation and removes HUD motion and inertia
