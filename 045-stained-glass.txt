=== DESCRIPTION ===
Vitrail is a stained-glass workshop set in a dim stone chapel. A tall Gothic lancet, an equilateral pointed arch clipped with an SVG clipPath, glows in an ashlar wall. Its glass is a Voronoi tessellation computed live in JavaScript, with each cell a pane of ruby, sapphire, emerald, amber or amethyst glass. Every pane has its own cut of a procedural sheet texture (mottled density, drawn streaks and trapped seed bubbles) and a gentle thickness gradient. Dark lead came with a highlight, solder joints at every junction and a heavy outer frame hold it together. Sunlight comes through the glass: the sun is a hotspot behind the panes, coloured light spills onto the carved stone, faint coloured shafts full of drifting dust hang in the air, and the window's image falls on the flagstone floor, sheared and stretched by the sun angle, with the lead lines casting thin shadows through the pool. The window frame is SVG stonework: a splayed reveal, concentric carved rolls lit from the upper left, a hood mould with label stops, voussoir joints, colonnettes with capitals and bases, and a sloping sill. A rose-window mode swaps in a circular window with radially symmetric glass, stone spokes, a carved hub and a cusped rim. The side panel is set in inscriptional capitals under an illuminated initial V, drawn in SVG with gold leaf, a diapered blue field and vine tendrils.

=== MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES ===
- Voronoi by half-plane clipping against the convex window outline, with nearest-neighbour ordering and an early exit; recomputed every frame while seeds move
- SVG clipPaths (objectBoundingBox) for the pointed arch and the circle, applied to the glass canvas with CSS clip-path
- Per-pane rendering: a radial thickness gradient toward the sun, then a shared 256px procedural glass texture (value-noise density, wavy streaks, bubbles), composited with overlay at a unique rotation, scale and offset per pane
- Lead came as layered strokes (dark channel, flat face, offset highlight), deduplicated solder joints, and a heavy perimeter came
- Light transport faked with affine maps: the window's colours are projected onto the floor with a sun-dependent shear and foreshortening, interpolated through 14 translucent slices to make volumetric shafts, blurred in a low-resolution buffer and composited with screen blending
- Colour spill on the stone tracery (blurred, masked outside the glass with an even-odd clip), a wall halo, a floor bloom, and a slowly breathing light level suggesting passing clouds
- Dust motes that ride individual sun rays from the glass to the floor pool
- Procedural ashlar wall with tooling pits and a flagstone floor in one-point perspective, plus a vignette
- SVG tracery built at runtime from offset arch geometry (same arc centres, larger radii); gradient-stroked rolls imitate carved mouldings
- Palettes: Jewel box, Chartres blues, Sunset and Modern pastel; colour families are spatially coherent through value noise, and in rose mode they follow the orbits so the symmetry holds

=== INTERACTION MODEL ===
- Click the glass to split a pane: the new seed is born inside the pane under the pointer and slides out to the click point while its parent eases away, so the leading reflows smoothly
- Drag any seed (handles appear on hover) and the leading and the floor light follow live; in rose mode, dragging one seed moves its whole symmetric orbit
- Right-click, or long-press on touch, lifts a pane out; "Relax leading" runs animated Lloyd iterations; "New design" draws a fresh cartoon; "Save PNG" exports the window with a transparent surround
- Lancet/Rose toggle, four palette buttons, and a sun-angle slider that sweeps from a low eastern dawn through noon to a western dusk, moving the hotspot, the floor pool, the shafts and the motes
- Keyboard on the focused window: N new design, R relax, M switch window, P cycle palettes, arrow keys move the sun
- Heavy light layers refresh at a throttled rate while dragging; animation pauses when the tab is hidden; reduced motion turns off the motes, the breathing light and the split animations
