TITLE: Patchwork — A Generative Quilt

=== PROMPT (exact prompt used) ===
Create "Patchwork — A Generative Quilt". Every visit (and every click on "Stitch a new quilt") generates a unique quilt made from traditional blocks — Log Cabin, Flying Geese, Half-Square Triangles, Nine Patch, Pinwheel, Churn Dash, Ohio Star — laid out on a grid with sashing and borders. Fabrics are procedural SVG patterns: gingham, calico florals (tiny flower motifs), polka dots, stripes and chambray, in warm cottage palettes (e.g. barn red #9e2a2b, mustard #e09f3e, denim #335c67, cream #fff3b0, sage #9fb8ad). Visible dashed stitching, slight puffy shading on each patch (quilted look via inner shadows) and a wavy binding edge. Interaction: hovering a block lifts it slightly and shows its pattern name; clicking cycles its pattern; a palette chooser (Farmhouse, Indigo, Sunset, Scandinavian); a "drape" toggle shows the quilt hanging on a line, waving gently in the wind. Download PNG. Typography: a warm serif with an embroidery-like dashed underline.

=== DESCRIPTION ===
Patchwork is a cottage-craft page that pieces a new quilt on every visit. It sits on warm linen and is set in a chunky warm serif with an embroidered, dashed underline and a spool-and-needle mark. The quilt is built entirely in SVG from seven traditional blocks: Log Cabin, Flying Geese, Half-Square Triangles, Nine Patch, Pinwheel, Churn Dash and Ohio Star. Each block is drawn from real piecing geometry in a unit square and turned a random quarter-turn. The blocks sit in sashing with cornerstones, a printed border and a wavy binding. Every patch is cut from procedural SVG fabric patterns: gingham, calico florals with five-petal flowers and leaves, polka dots, straight or bias stripes, chambray weave, flecked muslin and near-solids. The palettes are cottage-warm (Farmhouse's barn red, mustard, denim, cream and sage, plus Indigo, Sunset and Scandinavian). Patches get puffy quilted shading and dashed hand-stitching inset along every seam. A sewn-on label card gives the quilt a whimsical fictional name, a number, block counts with mini icons and its finished size, and holds the controls. A "Drape on line" mode hangs the quilt from wooden pegs on a sagging washing line under an open sky, where it billows in the wind.

=== MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES ===
- Seeded procedural generation: each quilt favours two or three block types, with the occasional surprise, and a role system (light background, dark, accent, medium) maps fabrics to patches so contrast reads the way it does in real piecing
- Fabrics as SVG <pattern> tiles generated per palette from its light, mid and dark tones (gingham by overlapping translucent bands, calico from petal circles, chambray from crossing hairlines, bias stripes via patternTransform)
- Quilted puff: an objectBoundingBox radial gradient laid over every patch, plus seam lines and dashed running stitches on a mathematically inset copy of each convex patch; a fine weave overlay covers the whole quilt
- Wavy binding built from alternating quadratic curves, border and binding stitch lines, cornerstones, and a soft blurred shadow under the quilt
- Hover lift with a CSS transform and an SVG feDropShadow filter; a pop animation when a block is re-pieced; a staggered "stitched in" reveal for new quilts
- Drape mode: the SVG is serialized into an image and texture-mapped onto a 22×16 cloth mesh with affine triangle warping on canvas; travelling sine waves and gusts, a sway that grows toward the hem, per-quad light and shadow in the folds, a catenary washing line and clothes pegs
- Mini block icons in the legend are rendered from the same block geometry

=== INTERACTION MODEL ===
- "Stitch a new quilt" generates a new design with a new name and number, and the blocks stitch in one by one
- Hovering a block lifts it and shows a fabric-tag tooltip with the pattern name and a one-line note; clicking cycles the block through all seven patterns
- Blocks are focusable buttons: Enter or Space re-pieces a block, and the arrow keys move between blocks
- Palette chooser (Farmhouse, Indigo, Sunset, Scandinavian) re-dyes every fabric in place
- "Drape on line" / "Lay it flat" toggles the waving washing-line view, which updates live if you change the palette or stitch a new quilt
- "Save PNG" rasterizes the quilt at 2× and downloads it; the page is responsive: a 4×5 quilt beside the label on desktop, 3×4 stacked above it on phones
- Reduced motion slows the wind and turns off the pop and reveal animations; the drape loop stops while the tab is hidden
