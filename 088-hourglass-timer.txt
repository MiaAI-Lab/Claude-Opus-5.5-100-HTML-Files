=== DESCRIPTION ===
Tempus is a focus timer built around a physically simulated hourglass standing in a desert at dusk. The sky runs from violet #2d1b3d to apricot #d98d62, with a low sun glowing through the glass, layered dunes, faint stars and drifting dust motes. The hourglass has a turned-wood (#6b4226) and brass (#c9a227) frame: two carved caps with grain lines and brass knobs, three turned posts with brass rings (the back one seen faintly through the glass), and brass collars where the bulbs meet the caps. Its SVG glass bulbs carry soft tint, inner shading and long specular reflections. Inside, every grain of sand (#e3b778 family) is simulated. The top pile drains into a conical crater, a thin continuous stream falls through the neck, and a mound builds below with grains tumbling down its slopes. Beside it is an elegant panel: a letter-spaced TEMPUS mark; a large, light classic-serif numeral (old-style figures where the font provides them) inside a ticked dial with a sand-gold progress arc; brass-rimmed preset chips (1, 5, 15, 25 min); a custom-minutes field; and Pause/Start, Flip, Reset and chime controls.

=== MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES ===
- One bulb-profile function (a straight 0.82-slope funnel near the neck, clipped by a sine-power dome) drives both the SVG glass outline and the sand grid mask, so the grains always sit exactly inside the drawn glass.
- The sand is a cellular automaton on an 85×201 grid, with per-grain colour, free-fall velocity (up to 5 cells per step, so the stream accelerates and thins), and two passes per row. Straight falls are resolved first, so voids climb vertically and the top pile forms a proper central crater. Then blocked grains tumble diagonally, with random friction for natural 45° slopes.
- A neck "gate" makes the timing exact. Each frame the target number of grains still above the neck is total × remaining / duration, and only the surplus may cross, so the top bulb empties precisely when the clock reaches zero at any preset. Pausing closes the gate and the stream stops.
- Time is anchored to a wall-clock end timestamp, with a 500 ms background watchdog, so the countdown and chime stay correct in hidden tabs. The sand catches up at full flow when you return.
- Flip: the whole assembly (back SVG, sand canvas and front SVG) rotates 180° with an eased CSS transform. Gravity reverses at the half-way point, and the remaining time becomes the elapsed time, exactly as in a real hourglass. Sand in the new top bulb then drains again.
- Rendering: each frame is written to a 32-bit ImageData at grid resolution and scaled up crisply. Per-column depth shading lightens the surface grains and darkens the buried ones for volume, and a continuous highlighted thread is drawn from the neck to the pile top while sand runs.
- Craft details: turned posts generated from a radius profile via Catmull-Rom to Bézier curves, brass gradients, wood-grain strokes, clip-pathed inner rim shading, per-bulb gradient reflections, and a ground shadow.
- The dial has 60 generated ticks with quarter marks, a gradient arc whose stroke-dashoffset tracks remaining time, and a glowing end-dot. It pulses when time is up, and the browser tab title mirrors the countdown.

=== INTERACTION MODEL ===
- Preset chips (aria-pressed) and a custom-minutes form (1–180) set the duration and refill the top bulb. Start/Pause toggles the flow. Reset refills. "Turn again" appears when all the sand is below.
- Flip with the Flip button, a click or tap on the hourglass itself, or the keyboard (the hourglass is a focusable role="button" responding to Enter and Space).
- Keyboard shortcuts: Space starts or pauses, F flips, R resets, 1–4 choose presets. Status changes are announced through a polite live region.
- At zero a gentle synthesized two-note bell chime plays: inharmonic partials with exponential decay and a soft delay tail, via Web Audio. The AudioContext is only created after a user gesture, and a bell toggle button (aria-pressed) mutes it.
- Responsive: side-by-side on desktop, and stacked on phones with the brand on top, the hourglass, then the dial and controls. Reduced motion makes the flip instant and removes the decorative drift.
