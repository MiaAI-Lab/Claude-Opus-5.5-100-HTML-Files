=== DESCRIPTION ===
MYTHIC is a fictional trading-card game. The page presents a single Holo Rare, Lumidrake, the Prism Wyrm (No. 077/250 from the invented Prism Tide set), like a museum piece under a soft purple spotlight on dark velvet (#0e0b16). The card is a real 3D object: it sways gently on its own, tilts toward the pointer on a damped spring, lifts as it flips to a guilloche-patterned back, and every foil layer reads its live rotation. The art is entirely SVG and mostly procedural. A faceted crystal dragon (a Catmull-Rom spine extruded into lit triangle facets, with shard wings, back spikes, horns and claws) sits over a forty-ray starburst, with a white beam entering its chest and leaving as a seven-colour spectrum, all on deep indigo. Around it is an embossed silver-pastel frame, engraved name and type plates, a pearl text box with an ability and an italic serif flavour line, ATK/DEF plaques, five rarity stars and the set number. Side plaques in a spaced serif and a floating pill toolbar complete the gallery setting.

=== MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES ===
- CSS 3D card: perspective, preserve-3d, two backface-hidden faces; rotateX/rotateY, flip and lift all driven by spring physics in requestAnimationFrame
- The live rotation is converted into CSS custom properties (--px, --py, --hyp, --bp) on each face (mirrored on the back), so every foil layer moves with the tilt
- Frame foil: a silver metallic gradient multiplied by a travelling pastel rainbow, plus an overlay cross-hatch "etched" texture
- Art holo: repeating rainbow bands hard-light blended with diagonal etch stripes and applied with mix-blend-mode color-dodge; its strength follows how far the card is tilted
- Glitter: two procedurally generated sparkle sheets (canvas to data URI) with parallax offsets so they twinkle against each other, radially masked around the light point
- Diagonal glare band (soft-light) that slides across the card, plus an overlay radial glare hotspot
- Foil lettering: the name is a clipped gradient that shifts with the tilt; the plates are embossed with layered inset and outset shadows
- Procedural SVG: faceted wyrm with hue running from cyan to pink along the body, wings and horns, starburst, star field and spectrum fan; the back uses parametric rosette (guilloche) curves and a sine-wave border
- Velvet room: a canvas-made nap texture in overlay blend, vignette, a blurred spotlight cone, dust motes that brighten inside the beam, a blurred conic rainbow halo behind the card and a contact shadow that shifts with the tilt
- Booster pack in the same foil language, with crimped edges and one shared jagged tear line (clip-path polygons); the Web Animations API runs the enter, shake, tear, rise, flash and settle
- Sparkle burst particles (pooled) on the flash, and a few sparkles on each flip

=== INTERACTION MODEL ===
- Mouse: move over the card to tilt it; the foil, glitter, glare and halo all respond. After a short pause the card returns to a slow idle sway
- Touch: drag on the card to tilt (touch-action none), tap to flip
- Click or tap the card, the Flip button, or press F / Enter / Space (card focused) to flip to the patterned back
- Arrow keys tilt the focused card
- "Open a pack" button or P: the card fades away, a foil booster springs in, crinkles and shakes, tears along its jagged seam, the card slides out, a white flash and sparkle burst follow, the wrapper drops away and the card settles back to centre
- Synthesized Web Audio effects (foil crinkle, tear, flip swish, bell chime) start only after a gesture; a speaker toggle mutes them and the setting is saved in localStorage (guarded with try/catch)
- Announcements through an aria-live region; animation pauses when the tab is hidden; reduced-motion mode stops the idle sway and motes, stiffens the springs and shortens the pack sequence to simple fades
