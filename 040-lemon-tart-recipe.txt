=== DESCRIPTION ===
A warm, illustrated family recipe card for a fictional grandmother's lemon ricotta tart. A cream page with a faint dotted-linen print opens on a two-part hero. On the left is a "Ricettario di famiglia" kicker, a tilted italic terracotta "Nonna Lucia's" over a heavy serif "Lemon Ricotta Tart", a short story and an at-a-glance strip. On the right is a hand-drawn scene: a terracotta arch with a lemon-yellow sun, a golden fluted tart topped with candied lemon slices on a plate, a green gingham cloth fading into the page, a whole lemon, a cut half and leaves. Lemon slices slowly spin on the tart's surface (in perspective) and float around the scene. Below, a sticky ingredients card on the left and nine numbered method cards on the right carry the same palette of lemon #f7d44c, leaf #5b8c51, terracotta #c65d3b, cream #fbf6ea and ink #2e2a24. The current step is outlined with a lemon offset shadow, and Nonna's tips sit in tilted italic margin notes. A print stylesheet turns the page into a clean black-on-white card.

=== MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES ===
- A hand-drawn illustration in inline SVG. One filter chain wobbles every line and fill (feTurbulence + feDisplacementMap), then composites a fine paper grain only inside the drawn shapes. There are flat colour fills, ink outlines, a gingham pattern, and nested linear-gradient masks that dissolve the tablecloth into the page on three sides.
- Runtime-built SVG details: the tart's scalloped fluted rim and wall flutes are computed around an ellipse. Each lemon slice (rind, pith, flesh, segment membranes, juice sacs) has slightly irregular hand-drawn edges.
- The animated layer is a separate overlaid SVG, so the heavy filter never repaints. Nine slices spin inside a scale(1, .36) perspective frame, so they turn on the tart's surface. Floating slices spin and bob, and a leaf sways (CSS transform-box: fill-box).
- Typography: an Iowan Old Style / Palatino / P052 stack with a rotated italic byline, a small-caps first line, wavy-underlined "Nonna's tip" labels in italic, and tabular lining numerals for quantities.
- Micro-interactions: circular checkboxes pop and draw their tick. A terracotta strike-through sweeps across the ingredient name via an animated background-size (it works across wrapped lines), and a lemon progress bar fills. Changed quantities bump. Timer rings deplete via stroke-dashoffset and pulse when finished.
- A tactile "pressed" button style with an offset ink shadow, an offset lemon shadow on the current step, and a print stylesheet (@media print) with two-column ingredients and no controls.

=== INTERACTION MODEL ===
- The servings scaler (− / +, 4 to 16) recalculates every quantity. Metric shows rounded grams and ml. US shows friendly cups, tbsp and tsp with ½ ¼ ⅓ ⅔ ¾ fractions and remainders (e.g. "½ cup + 2 tbsp"). Counts use halves, the tin size and in-step temperatures and thicknesses update, and a Metric / US toggle switches everything, including °C/°F in the method.
- The ingredient checklist shows a gathered counter and progress bar. Checks, done steps, servings, units and the chime preference persist in localStorage (guarded with try/catch). "Clear ticks" resets them.
- Each step can be marked done, which advances the highlighted current step. Steps with ingredient needs show chips with the scaled quantities.
- Step timers (30, 15, 20, 35, 12 and 60 min) can be started, paused, resumed and reset, and several can run at once. Running timers appear in a floating dock that jumps to their step. On finish, a soft two-note synthesized bell chime plays (Web Audio, created on the first Start) and a toast appears. The header's "Chime on/off" toggle mutes it.
- Cook mode is a full-screen, large-type view of one step. It shows a giant italic numeral, progress dots, ingredient chips, the tip and a big timer, with Previous / "Done, next" / Skip buttons. Arrow keys and Escape work, focus is trapped, and a screen wake lock is requested where supported.
- "Print card" calls the browser's print dialog using the print stylesheet. Reduced-motion users get static slices and instant transitions.
