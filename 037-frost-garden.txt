=== DESCRIPTION ===
Frost Garden treats diffusion-limited aggregation as a pane of night glass where ice grows in real time. Against a deep blue-black field (#020617 into #0b1d3a, with a faint frosted-glass grain) hundreds of random walkers drift as faint motes, and each one that touches the crystal freezes onto it with a brief flash. Every particle is coloured by when it arrived, from pale ice #e0f7ff through cyan #67e8f9 to violet #a78bfa, and a soft additive glow layer makes the dense heart of the structure look lit from within. Three growth modes give three different pictures: a radial point-seed frost, window frost rising in a forest of fronds from a glowing sill, and six-armed stellar snowflakes with 60° side branches. The title is set in thin, widely spaced capitals, and a slim frosted instrument panel with crystalline corner marks holds the monospace readout and the controls.

=== MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES ===
- Point and line modes: off-lattice random walkers (continuous random directions, so the square grid leaves no four-armed bias) moving over a grid occupancy map with a "near cluster" flag layer
- Hierarchical safe jumps: 8-cell and 32-cell block maps let a walker far from any ice leap to a uniform point on the rim of its clear disc, the exact Brownian first-passage distribution, so growth stays lively
- Walkers spawn just outside each seed's current radius and are recycled past a kill radius; the line mode spawns just above the tallest frond with horizontal wrap-around
- Snow mode is a separate engine: DLA on a hexagonal lattice with noise reduction (a perimeter site freezes only after several walker hits) grown from a tiny hexagonal plate; each frozen site is copied through all 12 symmetries of the hexagon in cube coordinates, so the flake is exactly six-fold with side branches at 60°
- Arrival-time colouring uses each seed's own growth clock, through a 64-step palette LUT of pre-rendered dot and glow sprites (Ice, Coral, Gold filigree); switching palette re-colours the whole structure from the stored particle list
- Layered canvases: a static background (radial gradient plus noise grain), a quarter-resolution glow layer accumulated additively and composited with mix-blend-mode: screen, an incrementally drawn crisp frost layer, and a live layer for walkers, freeze flashes, twinkling cross-glints and seed ripples
- Only new particles are drawn each frame; the full structure is redrawn only on palette change or resize, where the ice is re-centred rather than lost (a large change such as a phone rotation starts fresh)

=== INTERACTION MODEL ===
- Seed modes Point / Line / Snow (buttons or keys 1 2 3); each restarts the chamber with a small head start so the first frame already shows frost
- Click or tap the glass to plant another seed; in Snow mode a click starts another snowflake (up to ten)
- Stickiness slider (0.05–1.00): lower gives dense coral-like growth, higher gives lacy ice; in Snow mode it sets how many hits a site needs, so lower values give smoother, more needle-like arms
- Speed slider (1–10) sets how many freezing events happen per frame, within a per-frame time budget
- Palette buttons Ice / Coral / Gold (or P) re-colour everything instantly
- Pause/Grow (Space), Clear (C), and PNG download (S), which composites background, glow and frost into one image with a small caption
- Live readout: particle count, elapsed growth time, active walkers, percentage grown, and an arrival-time colour key; a toast announces when the pane is fully frosted
- prefers-reduced-motion halves the growth rate and hides drifting walkers and glints; animation stops while the tab is hidden
