=== DESCRIPTION ===
Sumi — Ink in Water is a quiet, full-screen ink painting that you make by letting drops fall into water over handmade washi. A small stable-fluids solver runs underneath. Each drop blooms as a feathered source whose ink thins as it empties, which leaves dark rims and paler centres. Its advancing front sheds irregular vortex pairs that curl into mushroom caps and smoke-like filaments. Dragging stirs the water, and a very slow divergence-free current keeps everything drifting gently. The ink is rendered as Beer–Lambert absorbance, multiplied over procedurally generated paper that has formation clouds, kozo fibres, bark flecks, grain and a deckle shadow, so overlapping colours mix the way pigments do. Thin tidelines form where a front thins out, and the paper keeps a faint stain of where the ink lingered. The page is framed like a hanging scroll. An upright vertical title "SUMI" sits over a sideways italic subtitle and a whispered verse. A carved red hanko reading 水月 (suigetsu, "moon in water") signs the lower-left corner beside a small colophon. A minimal serif toolbar on the right offers the four inks, "Still the water" and a sound toggle.

=== MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES ===
- Grid-based stable fluids (Stam): semi-Lagrangian self-advection, implicit Gauss–Seidel viscosity, pressure projection with a warm-started Poisson solve, vorticity confinement and a breathing stream-function current; it runs at reduced resolution (about 144×90 on a laptop, about 49×105 on a phone)
- Dye carried on a 2× finer grid with MacCormack advection plus a min/max limiter, so thin filaments stay sharp; slow explicit diffusion feathers the pigment
- Ink drops modelled as spreading sources: an irregular angular "finger" profile, a drifting and pulsing core, and an irregular vortex-shedding ring at the front, plus stray eddies from the impact
- Three-channel absorbance dye (Beer–Lambert, derived from each ink hex against the paper #f4efe6), upsampled in absorbance space before a lookup-table exp, then composited onto the paper with mix-blend-mode: multiply
- Tidelines drawn as a gradient-weighted iso-line near the thin edge of each stain; a persistent stain buffer records where fronts lingered; static paper-sizing noise modulates how strongly each spot takes the ink
- Procedural washi on its own canvas: two octaves of soft formation clouds, a fine grain tile, about 1,500 translucent kozo fibres as quadratic curves, bark flecks and a vignette
- Overlay canvas for water ripples with meniscus highlights, a falling droplet that shrinks with a sharpening shadow, and a brush-tip cursor ring in the current ink colour
- SVG hanko in haku-bun style: strokes cut from a mask, then roughened and worn by feTurbulence/feDisplacementMap and a noise alpha mask
- Vertical typography with writing-mode: vertical-rl: an upright title, a sideways italic subtitle and a letter-spaced verse column

=== INTERACTION MODEL ===
- Click or tap to drop the current ink, with a ripple and a soft synthesized plink; hold still to keep pouring, so the bloom grows; drag to stir the water
- Toolbar: Sumi, Indigo (ai), Vermillion (shu) and Pale gold (kin), with aria-pressed state; "Still the water" fades the ink, the stains and the currents away over a few seconds
- When idle, a droplet occasionally falls on its own, with its shadow converging before the splash
- Keyboard: Space/Enter on the focused water drops ink in a random spot; 1–4 choose the ink; S stills the water; M toggles sound
- Sound is Web Audio only, created on the first gesture: a rising sine plink with stereo pan and a low exhale, through a filtered feedback echo. It has a visible toggle, and the preference is stored in guarded localStorage
- Adaptive quality lowers the solver grid if frames run heavy; resizing resamples the painting so it survives; the loop pauses while the tab is hidden; reduced motion slows the flow and turns off idle drops, ripples and the opening overture
