=== DESCRIPTION ===
"Silicon" is a fictional development board rendered as one large responsive SVG and laid on a dark teal anti-static mat beside a datasheet-style control panel. The board is green solder mask with noise texture, a soft sheen and hatched copper pours. Its gold traces, routed at 45 degrees with mask clearance halos, connect a 64-pin QFP processor (U1), a TSOP memory (U2), a SOIC logic chip (U3), a DPAK regulator (U4) and a QFN USB bridge (U5). Around them sit a 25 MHz crystal, aluminium electrolytic capacitors with vent crosses and polarity stripes, carbon-film resistors with correct four-band colour codes, SMD parts, five status LEDs and a power LED, a 2x10 header, USB-C and barrel-jack connectors, a slide switch and a small OLED module. White silkscreen marks every reference (U1, R12-style labels, C4, "REV 2.1"), and a monospace title block sits in the corner. The page boots itself: the switch slides on, the power LED lights, warm-white power flows down the 3V3 trunk, the LEDs light in sequence, the OLED types a boot log, and cyan data pulses start racing from chip to chip.

=== MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES ===
- Board built from data at runtime: each net is a list of points produced by a small 45-degree router (horizontal-first, vertical-first and centred dogleg), drawn as a wide mask-coloured clearance halo, then three copper strokes (dark edge, gold face, offset highlight)
- Parts drawn from reusable SVG builders: QFP, TSOP and SOIC bodies with tinned legs on gold pads, epoxy gradients, pin-1 dimples and laser-etched markings; plated through-hole rings with specular dots; axial resistors with real colour bands; electrolytic cans with a sleeve stripe and a scored vent
- Solder mask texture from an feTurbulence noise filter over a diagonal mask gradient, a radial sheen, a glossy diagonal highlight, a blurred board shadow, hatched copper pours with stitching-via grids, fiducials and mounting holes
- Canvas overlay aligned with the viewBox for all light: pulses with tapered tails sampled along each polyline and pre-rendered glow sprites composited with "lighter", pad flashes on arrival, LED blooms, an OLED glow, and orange heat bloom with rising haze on overclocked chips
- Pulses are routed across the net graph: when one reaches a part it continues along another net of that part, so signals hop from chip to chip; power pulses stay on the power nets
- Overclocked chips get an animated feTurbulence and feDisplacementMap heat-shimmer filter and an orange outline
- The OLED is live SVG text: a typed boot log, then a dashboard with packets, core clock, temperature and current, plus an animated square-wave scope trace
- Datasheet panel with gold corner brackets, a physical-looking rocker switch, tabular meters and a condensed technical typeface paired with monospace

=== INTERACTION MODEL ===
- Power: the panel rocker, clicking SW1 on the board, or the P key boots or shuts down the board (switch slide, LED sequence, OLED collapse on power-off)
- Hover or keyboard-focus any part to highlight all of its connected traces (others dim, a warm glow runs along them) and show a spec tooltip with live status
- Click a chip (U1 to U5), or press Enter on a focused chip, to overclock it: faster and denser pulses on its nets, heat shimmer, temperature and current rise, and the red HOT LED blinks; click again or use "Cool all chips" to return to nominal
- Logic probe mode (button or L): the cursor becomes a drawn probe that snaps to the nearest trace and reads its net, endpoints and voltage (12 V, 5 V, 3.3 V rails, ground, 25 MHz clock, LED forward voltage, or logic HIGH/LOW with pulse detection); works with touch drag
- Touch: first tap selects a part and shows its tooltip, a second tap on a chip overclocks it; Esc clears the selection and puts the probe away
- Status LEDs react to traffic (bus, I/O), a heartbeat and the clock; the meters show supply, rail, current, core clock, temperature and packet count
- Optional Web Audio sound (off by default): a power chirp and an overclock whine
- prefers-reduced-motion: the board boots instantly, pulses move at half speed without haze, and switch transitions are removed
