=== DESCRIPTION ===
Grandmaster's Study is a complete two-player (hot-seat) chess game staged like a fine board on a green leather desk under a warm lamp. The board alternates maple (#e8d0aa) and walnut (#8a5a3b) squares with real-looking grain: maple runs horizontally and walnut vertically, as on a hand-made board. It sits in a deep red-brown lacquered frame with a gloss highlight, a thin brass inlay, gold-filled engraved serif coordinates on all four sides and four screwed brass corner fittings. The pieces are original Staunton-inspired SVG silhouettes in warm ivory (#f7f0e1) and ebony (#1e1b18), lit from the upper left with highlights, detail lines and soft cast shadows. Beside the board, a classic serif panel carries the title, a status plate, brass control buttons, a green-baize tray of captured pieces with the material balance, and a cream score sheet with a red margin, blue ruled lines, editable player names and moves in algebraic notation. On phones the board fills the width and the panel stacks below.

=== MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES ===
- Wood grain from two inline-SVG feTurbulence filters (horizontal and vertical fractal noise) blended with multiply and split between light and dark squares by a conic-gradient checkerboard mask; per-square tonal variation from seeded gradient angles; an overall board sheen.
- A lacquer frame built from layered gradients and inset shadows, with an inner brass fillet (stacked box-shadows), gold-filled engraved coordinates (text-shadow bevel) and reusable brass corner symbols with bevel gradients and slotted screws.
- Piece symbols (<symbol> + <use>) filled by per-side linear gradients; highlight and detail strokes read CSS custom properties through the use shadow tree, so one drawing serves both ivory and ebony.
- Pieces glide on transform transitions; the selected piece lifts; dragged pieces scale up with a deeper shadow; captured pieces shrink and fade; pieces drop in with a staggered entrance at the start of each game.
- Legal targets shown as soft dots, and capture targets as rings; the last move is tinted amber; check is a red radial glow under the king.
- Brass buttons with bevels and a pressed state; a paper score sheet built from gradients (margin rule, ruled lines, vignette) with a highlighter mark on the current move; a baize tray with an inset shadow; a fixed double gold tooling line around the viewport and a feTurbulence leather texture.

=== INTERACTION MODEL ===
- Full legal chess: move generation with check detection, castling (with through-check rules), en passant, promotion with an on-board chooser (queen, rook, bishop, knight; Esc cancels), checkmate and stalemate, plus draws by the fifty-move rule, insufficient material and threefold repetition. Move generation was verified with perft against standard test positions.
- Click a piece then a target, or drag and drop it; an illegal drop glides back. Clicking a selected piece deselects it.
- Keyboard: every square is a focusable button with a descriptive label; arrow keys move between squares, Enter or Space selects and moves, U undoes and F flips. Moves are announced in a live region in spoken form ("knight takes e5, check").
- Undo takes back one move at a time (pieces glide back and captured pieces reappear); Flip turns the board with a quick crossfade and re-engraves the coordinates; New game asks for a second click to confirm mid-game, then pieces drop back in.
- Synthesized wooden "clack" for each move (filtered noise plus a body thump), a heavier double knock for captures, two knocks for castling, a soft bell on check and a lower bell at checkmate or a draw. It starts on the first interaction and has a Sound toggle.
- Player names typed on the score sheet are used in the status line; with prefers-reduced-motion, glides and entrance animations are turned off.
