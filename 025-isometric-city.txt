TITLE: Pocket Metropolis

=== PROMPT (exact prompt used) ===
Create "Pocket Metropolis", an isometric tiny-city builder toy. A canvas-rendered isometric grid (12×12 on desktop, fitted on mobile) in soft pastels: grass #a8d5a2, water #7ec8e3, roads #d6d0c4, and buildings in peach #ffb5a7, blush #fcd5ce, mint #b8e0d2 and lavender #cdb4db, with gentle ambient-occlusion shading on each block face. A bottom toolbar places houses, towers, trees, parks, roads and water; buildings pop in with a squash-and-stretch bounce. Little cars travel along connected road tiles and tiny people stroll in parks. A day/night cycle slowly shifts the lighting (sky gradient behind, windows lighting up at night, glowing streetlamps). A stats bubble shows population and happiness (parks near homes increase it). Interaction: click/tap tiles to place; right-click/long-press to bulldoze with a puff of dust; drag to pan; pinch/scroll to zoom; "Random town" seeds a charming city. Persist to localStorage. Typography: a rounded, friendly sans.

=== DESCRIPTION ===
Pocket Metropolis is a small pastel city-builder that fits in your pocket. A 12×12 isometric island floats in a soft sky, with grassy tiles on a layered soil base and a blurred shadow underneath. Every object is drawn procedurally on a single canvas: gabled houses in peach, blush, mint and lavender with chimneys and doors, and pastel towers with window grids, pilasters and rooftop tanks, gardens or blinking antennas. There are round-canopy trees that sway, parks with winding paths, flowerbeds, fountains, benches and strollers, and roads that auto-join their markings, sidewalks and zebra crossings. Water tiles have sandy banks and drifting glints. Little cars keep to the right-hand lane of connected roads. A slow day/night cycle moves the sun and moon along an arc, tints every surface through an ambient-light model, flips side-lighting and shadow direction between morning and evening, and at night lights up windows, streetlamps, antennas and headlights. The HUD is set in a rounded, friendly sans and rendered as cream cards: a title card with a sun/moon dial clock, a speech-bubble stats card whose drawn face smiles or frowns with happiness, and a raised pastel toolbar.

=== MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES ===
- Hand-rolled isometric projection on canvas with a camera transform (pan and zoom around the cursor), back-to-front painter's sorting of buildings, trees, lamps, cars and walkers by depth, and ground/shadow passes drawn first
- Box primitive with per-face lighting: side faces take sun-dependent factors and vertical ambient-occlusion gradients toward the ground, plus a soft edge highlight; contact AO under building bases
- Directional cast shadows projected on the ground; their length and direction follow the sun's elevation and azimuth
- Keyframed day/night model: ambient RGB multiplies every material; sky gradients, twinkling stars, a sun with bloom, a crescent moon and drifting clouds
- Emissive night layer: lit windows (hash-seeded on/off pattern), lamp light pools and halos, antenna beacons and car headlights, composited with 'lighter'
- Auto-tiling roads from a neighbour bitmask: dashed centre lines, sidewalks and curb corners where the road ends, zebra crossings at junctions, and a roundabout on a lone tile
- Squash-and-stretch pop-in (volume-preserving scale around the tile base), with a cascade from the centre on "Random town", expanding ground rings, and dust-puff particles when bulldozing
- Procedurally generated towns: a meandering river with a bridge, two avenues and a cross street, towers near the centre, houses along roads, parks next to homes, and trees along riverbanks
- Inline SVG isometric tool icons, a rotating sun/moon dial, and an animated smile path in the stats bubble

=== INTERACTION MODEL ===
- Click or tap a tile to build with the selected tool (House, Tower, Tree, Park, Road, Water); building on the same type again re-rolls its look
- Right-click or long-press (touch, with a haptic tick where available) to bulldoze with a puff of dust; the Bulldoze tool also works with a plain click
- Drag to pan, mouse wheel or pinch to zoom around the pointer; hovering shows a dashed tile cursor and a translucent ghost of the building
- Stats bubble: population comes from houses and towers; happiness is weighted by residents and rises with nearby parks, trees and water and road access, and drops for houses crowded by towers
- Clock controls: pause/resume and 1×/6×/24× speed; "Random town" seeds a new city; the sound toggle controls synthesized pops and dust noise (Web Audio, created on the first gesture)
- Keyboard: 1–7 select tools, arrows move a tile cursor on the focused map, Enter/Space builds, Delete/Backspace bulldozes, +/− zoom, R for a random town
- The town, the day count and the time of day persist in guarded localStorage; animation pauses when the tab is hidden; reduced motion slows traffic and time and skips the bounce and glints
