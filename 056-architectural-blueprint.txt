Design "Casa Lumen — Architectural Blueprint", an animated technical drawing of a fictional modernist house. Blueprint blue (#0b3d91 to #134a9e with a fine grid of lighter lines) with white/cyan line work (#e8f1ff, #9fd3ff). The plan draws itself on load: walls first (thick lines with hatched cuts), then doors with swing arcs, windows, stairs, furniture outlines, dimension lines with arrowheads and measurements, room labels, a north arrow, and a title block in the corner (project name, scale 1:100, sheet A-101, drawn by). === DESCRIPTION ===
Casa Lumen is a drawing sheet for a fictional modernist house on blueprint blue (#0b3d91 to #134a9e), with a fine and a coarse grid, film grain, a double sheet border and zone letters and numbers in the margin. Its left side is the live drawing, in white (#e8f1ff) and cyan (#9fd3ff) line work. Its right side is a drafting column: a room schedule, general notes, a 1:100 scale bar and a full title block (project, drawing name, scale 1:100, sheet A-101, drawn by, checked, date, revision history). The house is designed to be internally consistent across three views. It is an 18 × 11 m single-storey pavilion wrapped around a glass patio open to the sky, with a 16-riser stair up to an upper studio and a roof slab that cantilevers 1.20 m over a south terrace. The South Elevation shows the same openings, and Section A–A (cut through the stair and patio) shows the noon sun at 75° and 28° for latitude 38.5° N passing under the overhang. Every view draws itself like a hand drafting. A Pencil mode re-draws the sheet in graphite on warm paper.

=== MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES ===
- All geometry is authored in metres and turned into SVG at 1 m = 40 units by small drawing helpers (lines, rectangles, rounded rectangles, arcs, dimensions, arrowheads, scalloped tree canopies)
- Staggered stroke-dashoffset drafting (pathLength = 1) in a real drafting order: walls and cuts, hatched poché, doors with swing arcs and windows, stair treads with a break line, furniture, dimension chains with arrowheads, lettering, then symbols; a progress rule in the toolbar names the layer being drawn
- Plan conventions: 45° hatched wall cuts, window symbols in the wall thickness, sliding glazing on two tracks, dashed treads above the cut plane, a dashed roof overhang, a section line with A tags and view arrows, a north arrow, room numbers and areas with paper-coloured halos, and a gravel-stippled patio
- Elevation: timber cladding strokes, glass reflection hatching, roof-terrace guard rails, the patio tree rising above the roof, a scale figure, level markers (±0.00, +3.60, +6.60) and dimension chains
- Section: solid poché for cut concrete (slabs, walls, footings, parapets, a sawtooth stair), earth hatching, glass cut detail, dashed sun rays with arrowheads for the solstice noon angles, and the tree seen through the open patio
- "Cross-drawing" view change: the old view erases (dashoffset to −1, reverse-staggered) while the new view starts drafting over it
- Pencil mode swaps CSS custom properties to graphite (#333) on paper (#f5f3ee), crossfades the background layers, turns on faint construction lines, and runs the ink through an SVG feTurbulence + feDisplacementMap filter for a hand-drawn wobble
- Technical lettering: condensed DIN/ISO-style sans stack in wide-tracked uppercase, with monospace for dimensions and title-block data

=== INTERACTION MODEL ===
- Floor plan / Elevation / Section tabs (arrow-key navigable) switch views with the erase-and-redraw transition; the title block's drawing name and sheet number follow the view
- Hover, focus or tap a room (plan), a space (section) or a glazing element (elevation): it fills with translucent cyan and a floating label gives its name, area in m² and dimensions; the matching row in the room schedule lights up, and hovering a schedule row highlights its room
- The Pencil mode switch (role="switch") erases the drawing and re-drafts it in graphite on paper; Redraw replays the drafting animation
- On narrow screens the sheet stacks: the drawing sits in a horizontally swipeable frame (scrolled to the centre) above the schedule, notes and title block
- Reduced motion shows every view complete, without the drawing or erasing animations
