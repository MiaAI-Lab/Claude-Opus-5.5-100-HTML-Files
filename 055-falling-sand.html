    How it works. Every 4-pixel cell holds one element. Each frame the grid is swept bottom-up, alternating direction, and each cell tries to fall, flow, rise or react. Denser elements sink through lighter liquids.

    • Sand sinks in water; oil floats on it.
    • Fire rises, burns wood, oil and plants, and boils water.
    • Steam rises, drifts and condenses back into rain.
    • Lava melts sand into glass and hardens to stone in water.
    • Plants creep into any water they touch.
