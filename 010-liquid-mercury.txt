TITLE: Mercury
FILE: 010-liquid-mercury.html

=== PROMPT (exact prompt used) ===
Create "MERCURY", a hero page for a fictional generative-audio studio whose centrepiece is a liquid chrome blob. On near-black (#050506), render a metaball cluster (canvas or SVG gooey filter) shaded as polished liquid metal — environment-map-like banding in silvers (#e8ecf1, #9aa3ad) and deep blacks with a thin iridescent rim (cyan → magenta → gold). The blobs orbit, merge and split organically; the cursor acts as a magnet, pulling a droplet that stretches and then snaps back with viscous easing. A giant wordmark "MERCURY" in a wide, heavy sans with a chrome gradient fill and a slow specular sweep; the blob passes behind and in front of letters (layering). Minimal navigation, a one-line manifesto, three glass "capability" chips, and a CTA button whose hover makes a small droplet drip from it. Interaction: pointer magnetism, click to split a droplet off, scrolling gently shrinks the blob into the corner. Tasteful, luxurious, silky 60fps; reduced motion shows a static sculpted frame.

=== DESCRIPTION ===
A luxurious hero page for MERCURY, a fictional generative-audio studio. On a near-black (#050506) stage, a live WebGL fragment shader renders a cluster of liquid-chrome droplets: a heavy core lobe with two inner lobes, six orbiting satellites on tilted 3D rings, a pointer-driven tendril and click-spawned droplets. The giant wide, heavy "MERCURY" wordmark is drawn inside the same shader as polished chrome with a bevel, a hard horizon line and a slow specular sweep, so the metal can genuinely pass behind and in front of the letters: satellites cross in front on one side of their orbit and behind on the other, while the core drifts through the letter plane over time. Around it, a quiet editorial frame: minimal mono navigation with a sound toggle, a serif-italic one-line manifesto, three frosted-glass capability chips, a chrome CTA that drips a droplet on hover, and accurate mercury facts in the corners (Hg, 80, 200.592 u, liquid from −38.83 °C to 356.73 °C). Below the hero, a studio statement, a list of fictional "selected pours" and a contact block; as you scroll, the sculpture shrinks into the bottom-right corner and stays with you.

=== MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES ===
- Single full-screen WebGL fragment shader (one triangle), sized to the viewport × devicePixelRatio (capped at 2) with a pixel budget and a conservative quality governor.
- Droplets as a smooth union (log-sum-exp) of C1 sphere-cap height profiles: every droplet is a perfect mirror sphere, and necks/fillets form only where droplets touch, with no seams between merged lobes.
- Analytic normals from the union's gradient, reflected into a procedural studio environment: silver sky (#e8ecf1 / #9aa3ad), a wobbling hard horizon, black floor, overhead rig band, three softboxes and dark pillars, rotated by pointer position.
- A thin iridescent rim (cyan → magenta → gold) on the silhouette plus a 2 px outer rim glow and soft halo; crease occlusion where lobes meet; a pointer-steered specular highlight.
- Wordmark rasterised once into a texture with three channels (crisp coverage, bevel blur, wide blur via the shadowBlur trick and additive compositing); the shader derives bevel normals, a chrome horizon gradient, iridescent bevel tint and a moving specular sweep from it.
- Per-pixel depth compositing: each droplet carries a z value from its 3D orbit; the metal is layered over or under the letters accordingly, with cast shadows and a dark contact line so the letters stay legible over the metal.
- Gooey SVG filter (blur + alpha threshold, composited atop) on the chrome CTA pill so its hover droplet necks off and falls.
- Glass chips with backdrop-filter, inset highlights and an iridescent masked border on hover; an iridescent gradient accent in the manifesto; dithering to prevent banding.

=== INTERACTION MODEL ===
- Pointer magnetism: near the sculpture, a five-bead tendril springs out toward the cursor (stiffer at the root, lazier at the tip), stretches into a neck and snaps back with underdamped, viscous spring easing; the whole cluster also leans toward the pointer.
- Click (or tap) anywhere that isn't a control to split a droplet off: it flies toward the click, coasts with drag, is pulled back and re-absorbed. The "." key does the same from the keyboard.
- Scrolling eases the sculpture down to the bottom-right corner at about a quarter of its size; clicking the corner droplet returns to the top.
- Sound toggle (nav) and "Hear it breathe" button start a synthesized Web Audio drone (open fifths through a breathing low-pass with echo) whose filter follows pull and pointer speed; droplets "plip" when they split and "gloop" when absorbed. Silent until you opt in; mute anytime.
- "Commission a pour" scrolls to contact; "Copy address" copies a non-URL address with a clipboard fallback and a toast.
- Live readout of droplet count and pull; rendering pauses when the tab is hidden; prefers-reduced-motion shows a single composed still frame with no animation.
