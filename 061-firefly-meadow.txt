=== DESCRIPTION ===
"Dusk Meadow — Fireflies" is a quiet, full-screen canvas landscape: a sky that slowly deepens from indigo #141b41 to a dusty-rose horizon #e8a0a0, stars that appear one by one as it darkens, a thin crescent moon with faint earthshine rising over layered hills, and a treeline of rounded canopies and spires in near-black greens (#0b1a12). A pale mist drifts along the meadow floor, and the foreground is a thicket of tall grass silhouettes swaying in a travelling wind. About 120 fireflies drift through three depth bands, blinking warm yellow-green #e8ff8a with bloom, and over roughly a minute they find each other and begin to flash together, lighting the whole meadow in shared pulses. The title is set in a delicate italic serif, with one accurate line about synchronous fireflies. A small, translucent control card in the bottom-left fades away when you leave it alone.

=== MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES ===
- Depth-ordered compositing: sky gradient → stars → moon → pre-rendered land layer → drifting mist (screen) → far fireflies → back grass → mid fireflies → ambient light → front grass with rim stroke → lantern core → near fireflies → vignette
- Time of day: the sky gradient is interpolated each frame between dusk and night palettes, a warm afterglow fades, and stars fade in by per-star brightness thresholds with twinkle; occasional meteors once it is dark enough
- Crescent moon painted offscreen: a textured disc carved with destination-out, a faint earthshine disc, shadow-blur halo and a large soft glow; it rises along a gentle arc over several minutes
- Land painted once per resize: sine-summed ridges with atmospheric colour steps, procedurally built treeline (clustered canopy circles, stepped conifer spires), and a rolling meadow whose distant grass catches the last light
- Grass: several hundred blades as tapered quadratic-curve paths, batched into one path per layer and filled with vertical gradients; each blade is a damped spring driven by a value-noise wind field with travelling waves and gusts, plus a radial push away from the lantern
- Fireflies: boids-style separation, cohesion and alignment plus wander, soft vertical bounds and depth drift; asymmetric flash pulses (quick rise, slower decay) drawn additively from a bloom sprite with a bright core
- Emergent synchrony: locally coupled Kuramoto oscillators (neighbours within a sight radius) plus a small global mean-field term, with coupling that ramps up over the first minute, so unison grows slowly instead of snapping; a live "in unison" meter shows the order parameter
- Collective light: the meadow gets a soft additive wash proportional to how many fireflies are lit at once, so synchronized flashes light the whole field

=== INTERACTION MODEL ===
- The pointer (or a finger) is a lantern: a warm glow that parts the grass around it
- Hold the lantern still and nearby fireflies drift in and circle it; move it quickly and they scatter
- Sync slider sets the coupling strength (0 lets every firefly keep its own rhythm); Wind slider sets the strength of the wind field and the wind sound
- "Crickets" toggle (off by default) starts a Web Audio scene: five synthesized crickets, each a sine voice gated into pulse trains with its own pitch, rhythm and stereo position, over a soft filtered-noise breeze
- Controls fade to near-invisible after a few idle seconds and return on pointer movement, key presses or focus; all controls are keyboard-accessible with visible focus rings
- The loop and audio pause while the tab is hidden; prefers-reduced-motion halves the grass sway, slows the fireflies, softens flashes and the shared meadow glow, stops twinkling and meteors, and slows the passage of time
