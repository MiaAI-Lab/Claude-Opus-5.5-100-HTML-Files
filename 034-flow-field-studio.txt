=== DESCRIPTION ===
Currents is a quiet generative studio: the artwork fills the window edge to edge and a slim, typographically calm side panel sits to its right (a pull-up sheet on phones). Thousands of particles ride a seeded 3D simplex-noise vector field and lay down long, hair-fine, semi-transparent strokes that pile up into silky ribbons wherever the currents converge, like wind maps or marbled paper. The field's z-axis drifts slowly, so the piece keeps evolving, and a very slow, periodic fade keeps it from silting up. Five curated palettes each re-theme the whole room: Kyoto Dusk (dusky violet ground, peach, rose, mauve, salmon), Glacier (pale ice ground, slate, teal, moss), Ember (near-black ground with additive orange light), Riso (cream paper, fluorescent pink and blue multiplied like a risograph print, with paper grain) and Mono Ink (sumi-black ink on warm paper). The title is set in a book serif with an italic accent, controls are labelled in understated monospace, and a museum-style caption names each piece ("Currents, No. 0860") from its seed.

=== MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES ===
- Inline, seeded 3D simplex noise (Gustavson-style) drives the flow angle, with a curl multiplier and an optional finer turbulence octave blended in
- Typed-array particle state (Float32Array positions, Int16Array lifetimes, Uint8Array colour) and one batched path per pigment per frame: particles are grouped into contiguous colour ranges so each colour is a single stroke() call with thousands of polyline segments
- Colour regions from a second, slower noise field with quantile thresholds, so pigments flow in territories like marbling instead of averaging into one tone
- Palette-specific compositing: source-over for Kyoto Dusk and Glacier, additive "lighter" for Ember, multiply for Riso and Mono Ink
- Periodic low-alpha background fades (every 24 frames) that keep trails long but avoid 8-bit ghosting
- Procedural paper-grain canvas layered with a CSS blend mode on light palettes and composited into the exported PNG
- Vortices as local tangential plus inward forces with a quadratic falloff; they are drawn on a separate overlay canvas with rotating dashed rings and spin arrows
- A time-boxed pre-roll and a warm-up phase with extra substeps, so the first frame already reads as a finished textile
- A cover-fit repaint on resize that keeps the painting rather than wiping it
- DPR-aware canvases (capped at 2); the whole UI re-themes through CSS custom properties with eased colour transitions

=== INTERACTION MODEL ===
- Click or tap the canvas to plant a vortex that bends the field locally; new vortices alternate spin, Shift reverses one, clicking an existing vortex lifts it (up to 12)
- Live sliders for noise scale, curl/turbulence, particle count (500–8,000) and stroke length, with accent-filled tracks and monospace readouts
- Five palette swatches (keys 1–5); a seed field (Enter to apply) plus a dice button for a new named seed; "Regenerate" re-renders the current seed reproducibly
- "Download PNG" exports the canvas plus grain via toBlob; a toast confirms the file name
- Pause/Flowing toggle, "Clear vortices", and keys R, N, Space, C and S
- On phones the panel is a bottom sheet with a Controls toggle; the choice of palette and seed is remembered with guarded localStorage; the loop stops while the tab is hidden, and reduced-motion slows the drift and substeps
