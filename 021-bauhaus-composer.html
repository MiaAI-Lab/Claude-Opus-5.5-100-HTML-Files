in 1923 wassily kandinsky circulated a questionnaire at the bauhaus asking people to fill a triangle, a square and a circle with yellow, red and blue. his own answer: the sharp triangle is yellow, the steady square red, the deep circle blue. press kandinsky to compose with those pairings.
