=== DESCRIPTION ===
"Windwalker" is a full-screen beach scene in which an original multi-legged wind sculpture, built from pale-yellow tubes with darker joints and topped by translucent billowing sails and a red pennant, strides along the sand. Behind it are a pale sky with a soft sun, drifting clouds, distant birds and a teal sea with rolling wave lines and a sliding swash. Every leg is a true Jansen linkage using the canonical lengths (a=38, b=41.5, c=39.3, d=40.1, e=55.8, f=39.4, g=36.7, h=65.7, i=49, j=50, k=61.9, l=7.8, m=15). Each frame, all joints are solved from circle–circle intersections of the crank angle, and the body is moved so the planted foot never slips. Red-pencil dashed curves trace each foot's characteristic path, footprints fade in the sand behind, and small sand puffs rise at each touchdown. The interface reads like an engineer's field notebook: a taped, ruled title label, a monospace readout strip, a graph-paper "fig. 1" diagram of a single leg with every length labelled, a control dock, and below the scene a notes page with the full table of link lengths and how the solver works.

=== MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES ===
- Linkage solver: crank tip from θ, then five circle–circle intersections (upper joint, knee, the outer corner of the upper triangle, the rear corner of the foot triangle, the foot), each on a fixed branch that was verified numerically to stay valid through the whole turn and to give the classic flat-bottomed foot path
- Mirrored leg pairs share a crank (solved at π − θ and reflected); each pair has a golden-ratio phase offset, and alternate stations sit on the far side of the spine, drawn lighter, smaller and offset for depth
- Slip-free walking: the lowest foot is the contact foot, the body advances by the opposite of that foot's motion in body space, and the body height settles so that foot rests on the sand, which produces the natural bob
- Tubes drawn as three strokes (outline, pale-yellow body, specular highlight) with glazed rigid triangles, darker joint caps, crank hubs, a braced spine and frame, and sails built from quadratic curves that billow and flap with the wind
- Pre-rendered sky, sun glow, sea gradient and sun glitter; clouds as layered radial-gradient puffs at three parallax depths; animated wave lines and swash foam
- Sand drawn with ground parallax tuned to exactly 1 on the walking line, so wind ripples, shells and pebbles (hashed per world tile) stay locked to the footprints
- Pooled footprints that fade over 45 seconds, touchdown puffs, wind-blown sand grains and a soft elliptical shadow
- Graph-paper diagram canvas: the leg to scale with labelled lengths on paper-backed tags, hatched fixed pivots, dimension lines for a and l, the crank circle, and the dashed foot trajectory with a live foot marker

=== INTERACTION MODEL ===
- Wind slider (0 to 12 m/s) sets the crank speed, the sail billow, the pennant, the cloud drift and the blowing sand; at zero the beest stops
- Plus and minus buttons (or + and - keys) add or remove leg pairs, from 1 to 8 pairs (2 to 16 legs)
- "Leg diagram" (D) opens the single-leg figure with its labelled lengths and foot trajectory; on wide screens the camera leads a little so the beest stays visible
- "Trace paths" (T) shows or hides every foot's characteristic curve and current foot position
- Drag the beach (mouse or horizontal touch swipe) or use the arrow keys on the focused scene to pan; "Follow" (F or Home) re-centres the camera on the beest
- Live readouts show crank angle, pace and distance walked (reading one linkage unit as one centimetre)
- Optional Web Audio (off by default): filtered-noise wind that follows the slider and soft tube taps at each footfall
- The page opens with a few seconds already walked, so there are footprints in the sand; prefers-reduced-motion lowers the wind, damps the sails and removes puffs and blowing grains
