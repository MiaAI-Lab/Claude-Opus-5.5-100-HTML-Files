=== DESCRIPTION ===
Plaza Aeterna is a fictional 1994 shopping mall shown as a Windows-95-style desktop floating in a vaporwave sunset. The sky runs from cyan through violet to hot pink, with a striped yellow-to-magenta sun, wireframe mountain ranges and a neon grid floor rushing toward the viewer. Two procedurally drawn palms frame a marble bust of Helios on a plinth, which turns slowly on its axis while pink and cyan light rake across it. Bevelled grey windows with navy title bars float over the scene: a Welcome.txt note with full-width ＡＥＳＴＨＥＴＩＣ lettering and a chrome serif-italic logotype, a tabbed Store Directory with pastel shop icons and a live floor plan, and a Jukebox MIDI player that synthesises lo-fi mall muzak. A taskbar, Start menu, VHS on-screen display and CRT scanlines complete the illusion. On phones the windows become a column of stacked cards under a full-height hero of the sun and bust.

=== MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES ===
- Pseudo-3D marble bust in layered SVG: the head is a rotational solid whose silhouette is recomputed every frame from per-height elliptical cross-sections, so the jaw and chin shift correctly as it turns
- Facial features (eyes with incised irises, lids, brow shadow, a two-plane shaded nose, lips, nasolabial folds, chin) defined as 3D curves on the head surface and projected each frame, fading out as they turn away from the viewer
- About 120 hair curls placed on a spherical shell, depth-sorted and swapped between behind-skull and in-front-of-face groups as they rotate; ears foreshorten from edge-on to full view
- Procedural marble veining: seeded random walks on the head surface (re-projected and back-face culled every frame) plus clipped static veins on the torso
- Torso with a himation drape and fibula brooch, narrowed by a projected-width scale as it turns; front anatomy and drape cross-fade into back detail (spine, shoulder blades) when seen from behind
- Canvas neon grid floor with true perspective spacing (y = horizon + D/z), a double-stroke glow pass, horizon haze, a sun reflection and low-poly wireframe mountain ranges
- Striped sun built from an SVG mask whose black bands scroll and thicken toward the horizon
- Palm fronds generated procedurally as spines with drooping leaflet triangles in a shared SVG symbol, swaying gently with CSS
- Authentic Win95 chrome from stacked inset box-shadows (raised and sunken bevels, tabs, trackbar, checkboxes, dithered pressed task buttons)
- Chrome serif-italic logotype using a background-clip gradient with a hard cyan offset shadow; full-width and katakana type in a CJK serif; mono labels with wide letter-spacing
- VHS layer: scanlines and vignette, a periodic tracking band sweeping down the screen, a PLAY ▶ SP on-screen display with a date stamp and live tape counter, and a per-window hover glitch (RGB-split drop-shadows, horizontal jitter, a noisy band that sweeps down the window)
- Jukebox LCD with a scrolling marquee, glowing time and chord readouts, an analyser-driven spectrum canvas and a 4-octave keyboard that lights the sounding notes

=== INTERACTION MODEL ===
- Drag windows by their title bars (pointer capture works for mouse and touch); double-click a title bar or use the maximise button to toggle maximise; arrow keys move a focused title bar (Shift moves faster)
- Minimise a window and it shrinks into its taskbar button; clicking a taskbar button restores the window, focuses it, or minimises it again; close buttons remove the window and its taskbar entry
- Desktop icons (Welcome.txt, Directory, Jukebox.mid, Fountain.exe, Recycle Bin) open or refocus their windows on click; the Start menu (keyboard-navigable, Esc closes) opens every program, toggles the VHS filter and offers a "Shut Down…" screen
- Store Directory: Level 1–3 tabs (arrow-key navigable); hovering or focusing a store lights its unit on the floor plan and shows its hours in the status bar
- Jukebox: play/pause/stop/previous/next, three synthesised tracks with real chord progressions (FM electric piano, detuned saw pad, bass, bells, soft drums, tape-wow LFO, convolution reverb), a volume slider, "Slowed + reverb" tape mode and Mute; sound starts only after a click, and the tray speaker is a global mute
- Wishing Fountain: toss a penny for a coin arc, a splash ripple, a chime and a random wish; the penny count is kept in localStorage (guarded with try/catch)
- Recycle Bin: "Empty" crumples the files away, and Mall Management then restores them
- Drag the bust sideways to spin it with momentum; hovering windows and icons with a mouse triggers the VHS glitch
- prefers-reduced-motion stops the grid, the sun bands, the bust rotation, the glitches and the marquees; on short desktops (under 830 px tall, e.g. 1366×768 and 1280×720) the windows switch to compact versions (tighter paper, smaller map, playlist hidden, with tracks changed via previous/next) and a layout pass stacks the Directory and Jukebox so no windows collide; on phones the windows stack as full-width cards, minimising collapses a card to its title bar, and the taskbar shows icon-only buttons
