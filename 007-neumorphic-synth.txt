=== DESCRIPTION ===
SOFT MACHINE (model SM-16, a fictional instrument) is a fully playable polyphonic synthesizer sculpted as a single slab of soft clay (#e4e8ee) floating on a matching clay page. Every control is raised out of or pressed into that one surface with paired #ffffff / #b8bec7 shadows, and the only colour anywhere is warm coral #ff6b5a, used for LEDs, knob value arcs, the tempo fill and the glowing oscilloscope trace. An OLED-like inset screen dominates the upper left: before any sound it shows a live, band-limited preview of the chosen waveform as it would leave the filter (Fourier series times a two-pole low-pass response, so resonance produces real ringing), then switches to the actual AnalyserNode signal once you play. Four rotary knobs (cutoff, resonance, attack, release), a four-way waveform selector, a play/tempo transport, a 16-step sequencer in four grouped trays and a two-octave soft keyboard complete the device. Engraved small-caps labels in muted slate, screws in the corners, a speaker grille and a power LED that flickers with the output level give it the feel of a real object.

=== MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES ===
- Neumorphism from one palette only: raised states use paired outer shadows, pressed states use paired inset shadows, and every control (knobs, segmented buttons, pads, keys, chips, slider) animates between the two
- Oscilloscope canvas sized to the container x devicePixelRatio (max 2) with a pre-rendered dot grid, a zero-crossing trigger for a stable live trace, and a three-pass coral glow stroke instead of expensive shadowBlur
- Idle "preview" trace computed additively from 48 harmonics of the selected waveform shaped by the current cutoff/Q, so turning the knobs visibly changes the wave even before audio starts
- Knobs built from inline SVG: an engraved groove, a highlight ring, eleven tick marks and a coral value arc driven by stroke-dasharray on a pathLength-normalised circle; the cap is a concave dish rotated through 270 degrees
- Floating clay tooltips that rise and fade on hover, drag and keyboard focus, showing formatted values (Hz/kHz, Q, ms/s)
- Engraved wordmark via light/dark text-shadow, CSS-only speaker grille, screw heads with rotated slots, and a procedural film-grain canvas behind the whole page
- Sequencer playhead lit sample-accurately from a ring buffer of scheduled audio times, with a pulse ring for the current step and a dashed "listening" ring for an armed step
- Phone layout: screen stacks above a single row of four knobs, the sequencer folds into 8 x 2 and the keyboard rebuilds itself as one compact octave

=== INTERACTION MODEL ===
- Play the keyboard with mouse or touch (glissando by sliding across keys) or with the computer keys A W S E D F T G Y H U J K (O L P ; extend upward); Z / X or the - / + chips shift the octave
- Sound path per voice: oscillator -> resonant low-pass (with a short filter pluck) -> attack/release amplitude envelope -> darkened echo -> compressor -> analyser -> master; polyphonic, voices cleaned up when they end
- Knobs: drag vertically (Shift for fine control), mouse wheel, arrow/Page/Home/End keys when focused, double-click to reset; changes are applied to sounding voices in real time
- Waveform selector is a radio group (click or arrow keys) and retunes playing voices instantly
- Sequencer: play/stop button or Space; tempo slider 60-180 BPM; tap a pad to light it and it "listens", then play any key to give that step its note; Shuffle writes a new A-minor-pentatonic phrase, Clear empties the pattern
- Audio context is created only on the first gesture; the speaker button mutes/unmutes with a short fade while the scope keeps drawing
- Settings, pattern, tempo and octave persist in localStorage (guarded with try/catch); the sequencer stops and held notes release when the tab is hidden; reduced-motion users get a still preview trace and no pulsing
