Monolith

Mass is a material.Shadow is a material.Time is a material.

MONOLITH designs in concrete, stone and earth for places with weather: northern coasts, high deserts and wet valleys. We use heavy walls to slow heat down, deep openings to control light, and landscape that is given room to take over. Every drawing here responds to the sun. Move it and watch the shadows swing.
