=== DESCRIPTION ===
Harmonia presents the classic pendulum-wave apparatus as a quiet piece of kinetic art on a charcoal (#16171b) stage. Eighteen bifilar pendulums (each bob hangs from two fine white strings in a V, so it swings in a single plane) hang from a brushed-metal bar seen in a slightly elevated three-quarter perspective. Their lengths come from L = g(T/2π)² so that they complete exactly 51, 52 … 68 oscillations in 60 seconds (34.4 cm down to 19.3 cm). Every bob starts from the same 5.5 cm throw and follows the exact small-angle solution, so the snake, the two-, three- and four-row splits, the chaos and the realignment at 60 s all come out of the physics rather than from choreography. The glowing spheres run through a spectrum from peach #ffd6a5 to ice #9bf6ff, and optional light trails sweep out translucent silk ribbons as the wave line moves through space. Beneath the stage, a monospace instrument console shows elapsed time, cycle phase, the named pattern, the neighbour phase offset, an orrery-like phase dial, and a scrubbable 60-second timeline marked with the pattern regions and the ¼ ⅓ ½ ⅔ ¾ split points.

=== MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES ===
- Hand-rolled 3D camera (yaw/pitch/perspective) projecting world-space metres onto a DPR-aware canvas, with an auto-fit step that frames the apparatus for any viewport and moves the masthead clear of it
- Closed-form small-angle solution θ(t) = θ₀·cos(ωt) with lengths from L = g(T/2π)², so the 60 s realignment is exact and every past instant can be evaluated directly
- Light-trail ribbons: the wave curve through all 18 bobs is re-evaluated at 58 past instants and stroked additively with a single peach→mint→ice gradient, fading quadratically with age, so the trails appear instantly even after scrubbing
- Brushed-metal bar built from back-face-culled box faces; the long faces use an offscreen streak texture mapped onto each projected face with an affine pattern transform, plus shading gradients, a machined edge highlight and an anisotropic sheen that slides as the camera orbits
- Pre-rendered sphere and glow sprites per pendulum (additive glow whose intensity follows each bob's speed |sin ωt|), depth-sorted every frame
- Soft light pools projected onto an implied floor plane beneath each bob, and ceiling cables that fade away with height and with camera pitch
- Phase dial: 18 concentric orbits with one dot per pendulum at phase ωt, joined by a hairline so the fan, spiral and split patterns are legible at a glance
- Timeline built from CSS bands (gradient snake, solid split, hatched chaos, white realignment) with a masked tick ruler and a glowing playhead
- Smooth camera easing between the elevated side view and a true top-down view; in portrait the top view turns the bar vertical to fit the phone

=== INTERACTION MODEL ===
- Play/Pause, speed selector (0.25×–4× in eight steps), Trails toggle, View toggle (Side / Top) and Chime toggle, all as labelled buttons with pressed states and visible focus rings
- Release: a translucent release board sweeps in and gathers every bob to the same 5.5 cm throw with a slight stagger, holds, then drops them all together at t = 0
- Timeline scrubber (pointer drag or keyboard: arrows ±0.5 s, Page Up/Down ±5 s, Home/End) jumps anywhere in the 60 s pattern, trails included
- Drag the stage to orbit the camera (horizontal drag only on touch, so the page still scrolls); double-click resets the angle
- Hover (or tap) a sphere to highlight it and read its swing count, period, length and live angle in a tooltip
- Optional chime: after a user gesture, each pendulum plays its own major-pentatonic bell tone (sine plus an inharmonic partial, panned by position, through a soft delay) when it crosses centre; muted by default and rate-limited
- Keyboard shortcuts: Space play/pause, R release, T trails, V view, C chime, ←/→ scrub, [ ] speed
- prefers-reduced-motion starts at half speed with trails off and camera moves become instant; animation stops while the tab is hidden
