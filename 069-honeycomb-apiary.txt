TITLE: Apiary — The Honeycomb
FILE: 069-honeycomb-apiary.html

=== PROMPT (exact prompt used) ===
Build "Apiary — The Honeycomb", an interactive hexagonal-grid artwork. The screen fills with hexagonal cells (canvas or SVG) in honey tones — amber #f6a821, gold #ffc93c, deep honey #c77d0a, wax #fff1c1 — with dark comb gaps (#2a1a05). Cells have a subtle inner bevel and translucency: some are filled with glowing honey (animated fill level with a meniscus shine), others are empty wax, a few are capped. Hovering sends a spreading ripple of brightness through neighbouring cells (delayed by hex distance). A handful of bees (small creatures with blurred beating wings) fly figure-eight "waggle dance" paths and visit cells, filling them over time. Interaction: click a cell to fill it with honey (drip animation); drag across cells to paint; a "Harvest" button drains the cells with a flowing animation into a jar counter; the grid regenerates on resize. A side panel explains the waggle dance in two short, accurate sentences. Typography: a warm serif title with small-caps labels.

=== DESCRIPTION ===
Apiary is a living honeycomb that fills the screen: hundreds of pointy-top hexagonal cells in amber #f6a821, gold #ffc93c, deep honey #c77d0a and wax #fff1c1, separated by dark comb walls (#2a1a05). Empty cells read as faceted wax tubes lit from the upper left. Honey cells are translucent and glow from within, each filled to its own level with a curved meniscus and a small specular glint. Capped cells, clustered toward the top of the frame as in a real comb, are pale domed wax lids. Seven bees with banded abdomens, fuzzy thoraxes and ghosted, motion-blurred wings trace figure-eight waggle dances whose dotted trails fade on the comb, then fly off to visit cells and top them up. A warm glass panel with a serif title and small-caps labels holds an illustrated honey jar, the Harvest button, live counts of honey, capped and empty cells, and a two-sentence account of the waggle dance with a diagram and a live readout of what the current dancer is signalling.

=== MAJOR VISUAL TECHNIQUES ===
- Hex grid in axial/offset coordinates with exact pixel-to-cell cube rounding and hex distance; organic honey, capped and empty zones from a smooth sine-noise field plus a top-to-bottom gradient
- Pre-rendered sprites rebuilt on resize: a faceted wax tube (six walls shaded by a light vector, beveled rim), a translucent radially glowing honey body, a domed textured cap, and a hex-shaped glow
- The static comb is baked into one base canvas; each frame layers honey (cropped from the sprite bottom-up, so the fill level is exact inside the hexagon), meniscus curves, caps and an additive 'lighter' pass for the honey's inner glow and ripples
- Brightness ripples spread by hex distance with a delay per ring, from hover, keyboard moves and an intro wave across the comb
- Drip animation: a stretching droplet falls under gravity to the honey surface, then the level eases up with a sloshing surface
- Bees drawn with canvas paths (legs, clipped abdomen bands, radial-gradient thorax with fuzz, antennae, three ghosted wing positions per side for blur) plus soft shadows; the waggle run wiggles along its axis, then alternating left and right Bézier return loops form the figure-eight
- Harvest stream: pooled particles fly along quadratic curves from each draining cell into the jar on a top overlay canvas, with fading tails; the SVG jar's honey level rises with an animated wave and the lid bumps when a jar seals
- A baked vignette and warm light falloff; the glass panel carries a faint hexagonal pattern

=== INTERACTION MODEL ===
- Hover: each new cell under the pointer sends a ripple of light through its neighbours, delayed by hex distance
- Click or tap a cell to drip honey into it; drag to paint many cells; clicking a full cell asks the bees to cap it
- Harvest (panel or mobile dock, or H) uncaps and drains every filled cell in a wave that starts nearest the jar; honey flows into the jar, which counts poured cells, seals jars and shows progress toward the next
- Keyboard: focus the comb and use the arrow keys to move a cursor, Enter or Space to fill, H to harvest
- New frame regenerates the comb with a fresh pattern; Bees flying / resting toggles the bees; the grid also regenerates on resize
- The live dance readout translates the current dancer's waggle angle and run length into the direction of the flowers relative to the sun
- On phones: a compact title bar with a jar counter, a bottom dock with Harvest and "The dance", which slides up the explanation sheet; reduced motion stops wing blur, instant-fills cells and slows the bees; animation pauses when the tab is hidden
