Why it feels right

Soft on the eyes, firm on the details.

Real-feel shadows

Every surface is two outer shadows and two inner ones, tuned as pairs so light always falls from the same top-left window.

Elastic by default

Springs, not durations. Components overshoot a little and settle — like a thumbprint filling back in.

Squishy, still accessible

Clear focus rings, real switches and radios, text that clears contrast, and a calm mode for reduced motion.

Five hand-mixed pastels

Bubblegum, butter, mint, periwinkle and peach — each with a lighter and a deeper partner for highlights and shade.

Layered shadows. A soft coloured shadow outside lifts each shape off the page, one inner shadow darkens the lower-right edge and another brightens the upper-left, and a radial gradient rounds out the middle. The icons use the same recipe as an SVG filter.

It can, if the shadows do all the talking. Squishy keeps text in a deep plum ink on pale surfaces, uses shape and position as well as depth to show state, and never relies on a shadow alone to say “this is a button”.
